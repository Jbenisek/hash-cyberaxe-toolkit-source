# Cellbook & trail-mask atlas

Part of the HASH$CyberAxe Toolkit. Snapshot: November 5, 2026.

## What it does

Joins scientific cell records, saved question editions and trail-mask evidence into searchable visual readers.

## What this adds to the workflow

Before: Separate cell dumps and XML/witness files that are difficult to compare by eye.

What we added: Adds stable record links, grouped search, source context and exact mask/witness replay to the underlying data.
