# Collision replay & construction labs

Part of the HASH$CyberAxe Toolkit. Snapshot: November 5, 2026.

## What it does

Browser tools for replaying a saved SHA-256 pair and constructing a one-round BLAKE3 pair while inspecting the states.

## What this adds to the workflow

Before: Reading a numerical witness only as a static dump.

What we added: Makes the calculation inspectable and interactive, with changed-input controls alongside the original result.
