# Round Climber & game evidence tools

Part of the HASH$CyberAxe Toolkit. Snapshot: November 5, 2026.

## What it does

Educational game and readers for exploring constraints and inspecting saved numerical game events.

## What this adds to the workflow

Before: A research task expressed only as a command-line query or a table of solver labels.

What we added: Makes the constraint landscape explorable and connects saved events back to inspectable scientific evidence.
