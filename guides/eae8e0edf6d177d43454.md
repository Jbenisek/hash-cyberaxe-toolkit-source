## Reviewed usage contract

The historical builder launches analyze.py, reads test descriptions and local_counts.txt, maps named tests onto cell records and writes records_q2.json. Its own verdict function differs from the analyzer's. The usage replay extracts only that reviewed function and never executes the file-writing top level.

The usage checker executes four small pure-function fixtures plus copied text parsing, not the whole historical builder. Native top-level work scans its inputs and writes records_q2.json; it would overwrite that output. No cryptographic experiment or full attack price is measured.

**Historical evidence boundary**: Saved labels and sums are not independent collision verification, an exhaustive necessity proof or a justified sampling law. The saved process resamples particles. A large sample count does not itself establish independent trials.

**Historical top-level dependencies**: The whole builder expects analyze.py, both tests.txt and tests2.txt, local_counts.txt and relevant out/*.txt files in its current directory. Use the isolated public replay instead of the native whole-builder command.

## Outputs and interpretation

**Four classifier branches**: EX1>0 yields VIOLATORS REACH THE EVENT and lever=true. Otherwise N1>0 yields NECESSARY for the final window; otherwise a nonempty alive list yields NECESSARY with the observed death row; otherwise it yields NO VIOLATOR DRAWABLE. All four pure branches were checked.

**C1985 label provenance**: For N1=0, EX1=0, alive=[16], die=17, the builder says NECESSARY (violators alive rows [16], die at row 17, never reach the window). The analyzer on the same saved log says NO-VIOLATOR. This is a traced transformation, not an unknown source edition.

**Historical record format**: The full builder writes a JSON array with hash, rows, object, question, labels, status, number, source, evidence, date, lever, gain_bits, notes and topic. It records known b19v/four-pattern reuse rather than a new lever discovery. Its notes truncate one local-count string to 300 characters.

## Failure modes

- Do not run the historical builder in a live research directory: its top level writes records_q2.json.
- COMPUTED and NECESSARY are historical output labels. Neither certifies the sampling distribution, a rule-of-three bound, the original enumeration, or a global SHA-256 attack.
- The source assumes recognizable analyzer stdout and may silently omit tests or fail if the child errors or its format changes.
- A batch-drop superset argument does not by itself justify transferring a statistical bound between different conditional samplers.
- The known four-pattern lever was already used. A lever flag is not a new discovery or a complete improved-cost claim.
