## Reviewed usage contract

B4E revises the probability-calculation layer of the four-round, 256-lane BLAKE3 walk. The useful methodological lesson is the direction of a numerical bound: for a convex integrand, an exact midpoint sum lies below its integral, whereas exact trapezoids lie above it. The new helper chooses trapezoids. Replaying its arithmetic and proving all surrounding probability assumptions are different tasks. The source retains its predecessor and jungjipdo attributions.

## Source inspection and available execution evidence

static-source-review; Fresh own-source finite kernel/recovery controls, exact saved-plan ledger and isolated Appendix A replay at fixed Tp and B; no default optimization or original collision campaign..

The compiler-only example was checked with a ten-second subprocess limit. The separate fixed-parameter Appendix A replay had a thirty-second limit. Neither ran the original campaigns or default planner.

## Declared inputs

**Unchanged target and representation**: The message is 32 zero bytes followed by little-endian x, one 64-byte block, standard IV, counter 0, flags 11 and four rounds, unit 846. The stored walk is f XOR q. Endpoints contain a 64-bit key and 16-bit trail ID; two recovery passes rediscover lengths then align and walk.

**Changed numerical layer**: Relative to B4D, _zq, _lossq, _b256 and plan change. The remaining 54 functions/classes match structurally. The helper uses 20,000 trapezoid panels, a positive safety multiplier, a revised cut-trail term and planning threshold P_MIN=0.390000001. Its fixed-width loss model remains N=2^256 and L=2^122/Tp.

**Fixed historical parameters**: The bounded replay supplies Tp=67 and B=1459662419041000000000000000000000000 directly. It does not execute the script’s top-level parameter search. Reviewed definitions and constants from the pinned Appendix A source are copied into a temporary module, and a thirty-second subprocess limit bounds the numerical replay.

**Source editions**: The distributed appA.py has SHA-256 af1130ec3340b9a5446d1b470f7b947589796539268920daebd5260d9916b402. The original hash differs only because of newline representation; normalized equality is checked. appA.out was checked from its pinned local original, not advertised as a downloadable catalogue record. The package proof contains the historical output.

**Safe compiler example**: The example below builds and allocates this edition’s circuit only. It avoids original --selftest/--stats attempts, whole-script Appendix A execution and package launch/build scripts. Do not use the reduced plan’s width as evidence that loss evaluates a reduced-width probability model.

**What the quadrature change establishes**: For positive q and lambda, z(u)=128.5u^2+2q max(0,u-uh) is convex. Therefore exp(lambda z(u)-u) is convex, so its exact integral on each panel is bounded above by the chord area. That elementary direction argument does not by itself prove every floating-point error margin or the success model.

**Remaining scientific and interface boundaries**: The package states a target-transfer premise H1, not a theorem about the fixed BLAKE3 function. The _B cache still omits Sb; a mocked threshold oracle demonstrates stale reuse after Sb changes, not an error in the fixed default. Negative packed fields still alias unsigned maxima. Synthetic graph returns are not real BLAKE3 collision witnesses.

## Failure modes

- An output labeled certified is not a substitute for an independent theorem and rounding audit.
- Keep the target-transfer premise H1 separate from random-function calculations.
- Do not infer reduced-width probability behavior from the helper’s name or the reduced search fixtures.
- Clear the run-length cache or use a fresh process when varying Sb.
- Do not transfer a retained-generator replay, adjudication status or price from a different edition.
- Validate external record fields and preserve shared-endpoint recovery preconditions.
