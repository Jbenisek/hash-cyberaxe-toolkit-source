# BLAKE3 original six-round compact edition: two capacity boundaries

Source: `research/blake3_r6/compact/pre/pkg/blake3/experiments/bsrho.py`

## Reviewed usage contract

The original six-round compact package and its pre copy have identical source bytes. Their 38,790-operation binary circuit is checked independently on this edition. The compact layout declares 244,215 code bytes and 76,231 endpoint records, but the permitted trail envelope and recovery constants expose separate accounting boundaries. These are reproduced source-model issues, not a replacement score or an actual overflowing target walk.

## Source inspection and available execution evidence

static-source-review; Own-pin finite digest/allocation, generated-code inspection, field packing, synthetic recovery and supplied-budget ledger. No default optimization or original search..

One fixed circuit construction/allocation, tested with a ten-second subprocess limit. No default numerical model, optimization or original collision campaign.

## Declared inputs

**Target used by this implementation**: Six-round BLAKE3, unit 1262, a block of 32 zero bytes followed by little-endian x, standard IV, counter 0, length 64 and flags 11. Some docstrings still say seven rounds. Executable ROUNDS=6 and UNIT=1262 determine this target.

**Masked walk and compiler**: The walk is f XOR q. A batch has 256 bit-sliced lanes, 38,790 binary instructions and 771 allocated registers in 20 compiler chunks. Packed recovery uses seven 36-bit lanes and 1,307 primitive operations.

**Ten-byte fields**: Use unsigned 64-bit keys and unsigned 16-bit trail IDs. The finite test accepts trail 65,535 and rejects 65,536. Negative -1 aliases the all-ones fields, so validate nonnegative integers before packing.

**Bounded entry point**: The example constructs and allocates the circuit only. Do not launch historical --selftest, --stats, a default plan, embedded-C builds or collision searches to reproduce it. Use a disposable directory containing the exact downloaded source.

## Outputs and interpretation

**Fresh finite equivalence**: Independent digest comparisons cover 256 fixed lanes and two known answers. Allocation/chunk equivalence, two rejected mutations, a two-step masked chain and packed recovery controls pass. They do not establish a universal hash-generator or attack-success proof.

**Trail-field capacity and budget envelope**: The declared capacity is 76,231 records. The permitted start-count envelope 256+76,231+1=76,488 exceeds the 65,536-value trail field. Plan construction accepts the envelope, while packing rejects 65,536. This is an interface/accounting gap, not evidence that an actual target trajectory reaches it.

**Large recovery immediates cross the byte limit**: Generated recovery contains eight large q-mask immediates. Under the source five/36-byte encoding, charging these as short instructions omits at least 8×31=248 bytes. The declared 1,048,509-byte search phase therefore becomes at least 1,048,757, or 181 over one MiB. The five-round edition’s 176-byte figure must not be transferred here. This is not a full repaired layout or new adjudication.

**Operation ledger and scoring model**: Source constants: step=38,790, filter=34, body=38,827, code=244,215 and table=76,231. Setup/search phases are 356,183/1,048,509 declared bytes. At supplied Tp=22 and B=10^35, exact ledger N=12497547512969349027986683043151417991961. This is not the optimized default or a success-certified price.

**Synthetic control flow**: Six artificial one-batch cases cover a returned pair, a miss and budget exits. Four common-endpoint recovery graphs take 5, 7, 4 and 2 oracle calls. Four packing roundtrips and two overflow rejections pass. These are interface controls, not real-hash collision witnesses.

## Failure modes

- This source uses 64/16-bit fields; do not silently transfer another edition’s 63/17-bit fix or plan guard.
- Do not count every instruction as five bytes when recovery embeds 256-bit masks.
- A permitted budget envelope exceeding a field is not proof of a reached real-hash overflow.
- Some source prose is stale; read executable ROUNDS=6 and UNIT=1262.
- Synthetic returned pairs are not real BLAKE3 witnesses.
- No earlier select-gate verdict or retained generator result is transferred to this binary source.
