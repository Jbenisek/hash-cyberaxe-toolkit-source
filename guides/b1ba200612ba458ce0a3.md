# BLAKE3 original compact edition: small gates, two capacity boundaries

Source: `research/blake3_r4/compact/pre/pkg/blake3/experiments/bsrho.py`

Public SHA-256: `18fab9b59af2c88e4c3966bc6ab785e14d6cdea735e34875432504ae76942a0a`

## Reviewed usage contract

This original compact package and its pre copy contain identical source bytes. The 24,422-operation binary circuit is not the earlier select-gate circuit. Compact encoding reduces declared code space, but this edition retains 64-bit keys and 16-bit trail identifiers. The later compact_u5b edition changes those fields to 63/17; that correction must not be silently attributed to this source. The source retains its historical predecessor attributions.

## Source inspection and available execution evidence

static-source-review; Own-pin finite digest/allocation, generated-code inspection, field packing, synthetic recovery and supplied-budget ledger. No default optimization or original search..

One fixed circuit construction/allocation, tested with a ten-second subprocess limit. No default numerical model, optimization or original collision campaign.

## Declared inputs

**Target used by this implementation**: Four-round BLAKE3 with unit 846: a full block of 32 zero bytes followed by little-endian x, standard IV, counter 0, length 64 and flags 11. The source header retains older round/unit wording; ROUND=4 and UNIT=846 drive this implementation.

**Masked walk and compiler**: The walk is f XOR q. A batch has 256 bit-sliced lanes, 24,422 binary instructions and 771 allocated registers in 13 compiler chunks. Packed recovery uses seven 36-bit lanes and 836 primitive operations.

**Ten-byte fields**: Use unsigned 64-bit keys and unsigned 16-bit trail IDs. The finite test accepts trail 65,535 and rejects 65,536. Negative -1 aliases the all-ones fields, so validate nonnegative integers before packing.

**Bounded entry point**: The example constructs and allocates the circuit only. Do not launch historical --selftest, --stats, a default plan, embedded-C builds or collision searches to reproduce it. Use a disposable directory containing the exact downloaded source.

## Outputs and interpretation

**Fresh finite equivalence**: Independent digest comparisons cover 256 fixed lanes and two known answers. Allocation/chunk equivalence, two rejected mutations, a two-step masked chain and packed recovery controls pass. They do not establish a universal hash-generator or attack-success proof.

**Trail-field capacity and budget envelope**: The declared capacity is 83,650 records. The permitted start-count envelope is 256+83,650+1=83,907, exceeding the 65,536-value trail field. The plan construction accepts that envelope, while packing rejects 65,536. This is a concrete interface/accounting gap, not evidence that an actual target trajectory reaches the overflowing value.

**Large recovery immediates cross the byte limit**: Generated recovery contains eight large q-mask immediates. Under the source five/36-byte encoding, charging these as short instructions omits at least 8×31=248 bytes. The declared 1,048,504-byte search phase becomes at least 1,048,752, or 176 over 1 MiB. This lower bound is not a complete repaired layout or a new adjudication.

**Operation ledger and scoring model**: The source constants are step=24,422, handler=34, body=24,459, code=170,020 and table=83,650. Setup/search phases are 267,620/1,048,504 declared bytes. At supplied Tp=22 and B=100000000000000000000000000000000000, the integer ledger gives N=8101160960899879977153686755510001622100. This fixture is not the optimized default price; the capacity boundaries remain attached.

**Synthetic control flow**: Six artificial one-batch cases cover a returned pair, a miss and budget exits. Four common-endpoint recovery graphs take 5, 7, 4 and 2 oracle calls. Four packing roundtrips and two overflow rejections pass. These are interface controls, not real-hash collision witnesses.

## Failure modes

- Do not transfer the later 63/17-bit layout or its plan guard to this 64/16-bit edition.
- Do not count every instruction as five bytes when recovery embeds 256-bit masks.
- A permitted budget envelope exceeding a field is not proof of a reached real-hash overflow.
- The source header is stale; read the executable ROUND and UNIT constants.
- Synthetic returned pairs are not real BLAKE3 witnesses.
- No earlier select-gate verdict or retained generator result is transferred to this binary source.
