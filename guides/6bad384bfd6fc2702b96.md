## Reviewed usage contract

Create a derived characteristic with selected E rows relaxed to ?. This removes the companion screen's detected windows; it does not establish satisfiability or recover a collision.

## Source inspection and available execution evidence

static-source-review; seven-synthetic-pin-tool-cases-passed; originals-unchanged-in-fixtures.

Repeated scans of a small text characteristic. No measured campaign speedup or discovery price.

## Declared inputs

**CLI**: python fix_pins.py OUTDIR XML... . Use a dedicated empty output directory; existing derived filenames may be overwritten.

**Rows**: Same narrow parser as pin_screen.py. For the first flagged row r, replaces E_(r-4) with ? symbols of the original width and repeats.

## Outputs and interpretation

**Derived file**: OUTDIR/<basename minus its final four characters>_fx.xml. Assumes a four-character extension. Equal basenames from different directories can overwrite one another.

**Freed row list**: Printed E-row indices record changed constraints. The result is a relaxed question, not a witness or a proof of closure.

## Imports

## Worked examples

### Relax the disposable pin fixture

Working directory: Scratch directory containing fixture.xml from the pin-screen guide and both scripts

```text
python fix_pins.py derived fixture.xml
python pin_screen.py derived/fixture_fx.xml
```

Expected: First: freed E rows [0] and fixture_fx.xml. Then: ok and fixture_fx.xml. Only E row 0 is relaxed; fixture.xml is unchanged. Neither line proves SAT.

## Failure modes

- Use fresh output paths to avoid overwriting derived files.
- Malformed and duplicate rows are not comprehensively rejected.
- A ? bit permits equality as well as difference.
- The freed old h word may still be constrained by the complete schedule and IV.
