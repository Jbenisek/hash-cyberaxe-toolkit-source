# Power-of-two convolution counter: valid windows and complete batching

Source: `research/levers_1029/pos_r15_xconv/tools/xconv1.py`

Public SHA-256: `88535ee7467250a93ce83597feb0c1feeae3f81291043f234b879662fae94e01`

## Reviewed usage contract

Price the historical power-of-two, windowed group-buffer convolution layout: pruned first and last stages, two middle pass runs and a central stage. The code evaluates a model; it neither computes a convolution nor proves the surrounding attack's probability.

## Source inspection and available execution evidence

static-source-review; 360-boundary-parameter-triples-and-copied-cli-controls-passed; full-transform-not-executed.

A small integer DP plus a constant-size boundary partition. The 360 tested boundary triples used L=16,32,64,128, all positive K<=L/2 and cnt=1,3,8; they establish agreement with a per-index sum under the same inherited charges, not correctness of every charge premise.

## Declared inputs

**API domain**: group(L,K,cnt) returns component counts; phase(L,K,cnt,pos,margin=0) returns (total, group). Use integer power-of-two L>=16, 1<=K<=L/2, positive cnt and pos, nonnegative margin, and pos divisible by cnt. Check these at the caller; the original assertions cover only some conditions.

**Meaning of K**: K is the input support/window parameter passed into this counter, not automatically the baseline ledger's K. The published r15 extension calls phase(262144,111024,1024,2147483648,margin=1048576), with the second argument equal to twice the baseline K=55512.

**Companions and assumptions**: Imports F, oc and runsplit from geo.py and ROW/VD_GROUP from xconv.py. All three files must be present at the pinned versions. The middle-pass offset is omitted from the twiddle saving; the intended historical upper-bound reasoning uses the historical counts and charge signs, not arbitrary positive parameters.

## Outputs and interpretation

**Component dictionary**: group returns first, run, split, mid, last and tot, with tot=first+2*run+mid+last. last1 prices precisely the requested coefficient positions K..2K-1 under the given layout.

**Phase total**: Let ng=pos//cnt. phase returns 2*(7*K+2)+ng*(VD_GROUP+group.tot+margin)+3*ng+2. No extra partial group is charged, which is why divisibility is required.

**Original CLI**: Running xconv1.py prints a fixed historical model comparison using L=131072, K=55512 and cnt=1024. This main-program example is not the doubled-input extension row in the publication kit.

## Failure modes

- phase(64,12,8,32) and phase(64,12,8,39) return the same result: the extra seven positions are silently dropped. Both companion phase counters have this behavior.
- cnt=0 raises ZeroDivisionError. Nonpositive parameters and very short lengths are not completely validated.
- The power-of-two and window assertions disappear under -O. The teaching replay rejects -O; the original source is not a validated general-purpose API.
- The mixed-radix companion xconv.group uses n=L//3 and accepts both L=48 and L=49 with identical output for the tested other parameters. Require exact L=3*2^a before using that companion; its current assertions do not enforce divisibility by three.
- The fixed published ledgers satisfy the required lengths and complete-batch conditions. These reuse defects do not invalidate their independently reproduced integer totals.
- Exact integer output and agreement with a historical ledger do not certify a transform implementation, transcendental rounding bound, full memory law or attack success probability.
