# BLAKE3 original seven-round compact edition: inspect fields and code

Source: `research/blake3_r7/compact/pkg/blake3/experiments/bsrho.py`

## Reviewed usage contract

The original seven-round compact source and its pre copy have identical bytes. Fresh fixtures check its 45,974-operation binary circuit and 1,540-operation packed recovery. Its 281,300-byte declared code and 72,522 ten-byte records leave two distinct representation gaps. The seven-round numerical helper also changes; none of its success-law terms is certified by this finite replay.

## Source inspection and available execution evidence

static-source-review; Own-pin finite digest/allocation, generated-code inspection, field packing, synthetic recovery and supplied-budget ledger. No default optimization or original search..

One fixed circuit construction/allocation, tested with a ten-second subprocess limit. No default numerical model, optimization or original collision campaign.

## Declared inputs

**Target used by this implementation**: Seven-round BLAKE3, reference unit 1470, standard IV, counter 0, block length 64 and flags 11. The message is 32 zero bytes followed by little-endian 256-bit x. Executable ROUNDS=7 and UNIT=1470 define this edition.

**Masked walk and compiler**: The walk is f XOR q. A batch has 256 bit-sliced lanes, 45,974 binary instructions and 771 allocated registers in 23 compiler chunks. Packed recovery uses seven 36-bit lanes and 1,540 primitive operations.

**Ten-byte fields**: Use unsigned 64-bit keys and unsigned 16-bit trail IDs. The finite test accepts trail 65,535 and rejects 65,536. Negative -1 aliases the all-ones fields, so validate nonnegative integers before packing.

**Bounded entry point**: The example constructs and allocates the circuit only. Do not launch historical --selftest, --stats, a default plan, embedded-C builds or collision searches to reproduce it. Use a disposable directory containing the exact downloaded source.

## Outputs and interpretation

**Fresh finite equivalence**: Independent digest comparisons cover 256 fixed lanes and two known answers. Allocation/chunk equivalence, two rejected mutations, a two-step masked chain and packed recovery controls pass. They do not establish a universal hash-generator or attack-success proof.

**Trail-field capacity and budget envelope**: The declared table holds 72,522 records. The permitted start envelope 256+72,522+1=72,779 exceeds the 65,536 values of a 16-bit trail field. Packing rejects 65,536 although plan construction accepts the envelope. This is an accounting/interface gap, not evidence of an actual overflowing target trajectory.

**Large recovery immediates cross the byte limit**: Eight large recovery-mask immediates need at least 8×31=248 additional bytes under the declared five/36-byte encoding. Declared search memory 1,048,504 therefore becomes at least 1,048,752, or 176 bytes over one MiB. The six-round edition’s 181-byte excess must not be transferred here. This is not a complete repaired layout or a new adjudication.

**Operation ledger and scoring model**: Source constants: step=45,974, filter=34, body=46,011, code=281,300 and table=72,522. Declared setup/search phases: 400,452/1,048,504 bytes. At supplied Tp=22, kappa=13 and B=10^35, exact N=14680031730872080002177952968884962614324. This is an artificial supplied-budget fixture, not the optimized default or a success-certified attack price.

**Synthetic control flow**: Six artificial one-batch cases cover a returned pair, a miss and budget exits. Four common-endpoint recovery graphs take 5, 7, 4 and 2 oracle calls. Four packing roundtrips and two overflow rejections pass. These are interface controls, not real-hash collision witnesses.

**Changed numerical helper**: The tiny-loss exponential changes from -300+(exp(5)-1)hh to -120+(exp(2)-1)hh. Neither numerical helper nor optimizer was executed or proved. Finite circuit, field and ledger checks do not establish this probability premise.

## Failure modes

- This source uses 64/16-bit fields; do not silently transfer another edition’s 63/17-bit fix or plan guard.
- Do not count every instruction as five bytes when recovery embeds 256-bit masks.
- A permitted budget envelope exceeding a field is not proof of a reached real-hash overflow.
- Read this edition’s executable ROUNDS=7 and UNIT=1470; earlier-round field and byte totals do not transfer.
- Synthetic returned pairs are not real BLAKE3 witnesses.
- No earlier select-gate verdict or retained generator result is transferred to this binary source.
