# BLAKE3 b3r3t: inspect trial gates and observation counters

Source: `research/blake3_r3/b3r3t/pkg/blake3/experiments/rho.py`

Public SHA-256: `ce41f4e09ddacc2064f863693e54ad090010e034cb517b37225d2526632e341a`

## Reviewed usage contract

This single-block three-round BLAKE3 edition adds named 32-bit and 40-bit mixed-predicate experiments to the grouped distinguished-point search. Its routing, skipping and observation fields affect how results must be counted. The program also describes word-rounded table storage. These interfaces and finite arithmetic properties are reviewed here; no sampled success law, full-output collision or historical price is newly certified.

## Source inspection and available execution evidence

static-source-review; copied 70-input helper, independent digests, exact declared-ledger/layout arithmetic and mocked/synthetic interface controls; no original hash walk.

Finite circuit generation; 10 packed batches and 70 scalar checks; then 2,048 numerical threshold candidates for memory_layout()'s plan. No original cryptographic walk, --h1 campaign, CLI equivalence search or full attack is run. The layout uses exact integer rounding, while candidate selection uses floating-point tail calculations.

## Declared inputs

**Single-block context**: The message is 64 bytes: 32 zero bytes followed by the little-endian 256-bit chain value x. Standard IV, counter zero, length 64, flags 11, three rounds. This is not b3r3lb's 128-byte fixed-prefix family. Seven lanes occupy 36-bit slots.

**Named experiments**: rho-n32-a, rho-n32-b and rho-n32-c choose n=32, key=32m and trial-ID cutoff 24. rho-n40-a through rho-n40-d choose n=40, key=40m and cutoff 2. Each eligible trial gets one attempt. Both mixed profiles use U=16, W=4, Tp=12; s1 is 8 or 12 respectively. The full-width profile instead uses s1=32, W=90 and source-selected Tp=400.

**The cutoff is a numeric-ID comparison**: The executable condition is trial < cutoff, not request position and not the number of attempts already made. Negative trial IDs execute, duplicate eligible IDs execute repeatedly, and reordered IDs keep their own decisions. IDs at or above the cutoff return null messages with observations={attempts:0}. Thus arbitrary caller IDs do not enforce a 24- or 2-attempt per-request maximum. String IDs raise TypeError for recognized experiment names.

**Fallback and unvalidated metadata**: A missing or unknown experiment_id, including a misspelling, falls back to run_trial at n=22 with up to 60 attempts. schema_version, target_profile and event are not target validation. Trial identifiers are copied into responses; seeds are string-formatted with the attempt index and hashed with SHAKE-256.

**Do not confuse helpers with campaigns**: Import generates a finite arithmetic circuit. The imported selftest() tests 70 inputs. CLI --selftest also runs 24 paired search comparisons, 48 original search calls. CLI --h1 defaults to 60 mixed 32-bit attempts and six mixed 40-bit attempts. Neither campaign was run for this guide. The documented command calls only selftest() and memory_layout().

## Outputs and interpretation

**Skipped, halted and completed attempts**: Skipped numeric IDs return attempts=0. A budget halt returns attempts=1 and halted=1, but batches, stage2, finished and replays all reset to zero. Normal mixed attempts copy those four counters; cut is not copied. A halt's zeros do not establish zero work. Default run_trial likewise omits halted-attempt batches. Mocked controls exercised all seven names and these separate outcomes.

**Reported batches versus actual calls**: Normal batch observations still omit some speculative group, replay and recovery evaluations. Eight artificial constant-map fixtures preserve grouped/per-batch outcomes while exposing more actual batch calls than reported batches. These are control-flow tests, not BLAKE3 collision witnesses. A non-null mixed result targets only its selected 32 or 40 bits; the fallback targets 22 bits.

**Memory is exactly full after rounding**: The full-width table has 26,791 entries of 25 bytes: 669,775 bytes, rounded to 20,931 whole 256-bit words. The rounding adds 17 bytes. Code, counters and register storage bring the total to exactly 1,048,576 bytes (1 MiB), not the 1,048,559 unrounded figure. The field named total_in_words actually contains bytes. This is the source's abstract layout, not Python container size or process RSS.

**Separate plans from ledger prices**: plan(32,32m) and plan(40,40m) have L=1024/3 and 16384/3 respectively, whereas their default-key plans use a different predicate. ledger(32) and ledger(40) do not accept a mixed key: they price their default profiles, not the named experiments. Full ledger terms use exact rational arithmetic after floating-point selection. Numerical loss estimates are not interval-certified probability proofs.

**Kernel scope**: This source has 595 packed instructions and 25 live kernel values under dying-operand reuse, or 34 including saved group inputs and filter accumulator. Kernel spill counts at R=64,32,24,20,16 are 0,0,3,16,39. Zero kernel spills at R=32 does not certify the full grouped loop fits 32 registers. Seven independent digest fixtures and the 70-input helper pass; no complete allocation is verified.

**Meaning of the statistical report**: --h1 computes its own reduced-width success fractions and floating-point comparison statistics after running attempts. Its sampling law and full-field extrapolation are not proved by printing a z-score. Eligible-ID skips and halts must remain separate in any denominator, and repeated IDs or seeds must not silently become independent samples.

## Failure modes

- Do not treat request order as the attempt budget. Numeric ID, including sign and duplicates, decides whether a named-mode attempt runs.
- An unknown experiment name silently chooses the default 22-bit route; target-profile metadata does not prevent that fallback.
- A null pair can mean skipped, budget-halted or completed without a pair. Read attempts and halted before aggregating results.
- Zero counters after Halt do not mean no operations were performed. Normal batch counters are not complete work ledgers either.
- Rounded storage leaves no unused bytes under this source's 1 MiB abstract cap; do not quote the unrounded 17-byte margin.
- ledger(n) does not price the mixed 32m or 40m experiment just because the bit width matches.
- CLI --h1 and CLI --selftest launch searches; the documented imported-helper command does not.
- Finite digest agreement, a declared ledger and reduced-width statistics do not prove full-width success or historical acceptance.
