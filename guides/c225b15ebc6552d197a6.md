# Fresh-seed comparison: recompute means, quantiles and cost transfers

## Reviewed usage contract

Summarize the saved r37 baseline and p16 estimator outputs. Computes linear means, Student-style lower estimates and a paired delta-method log-ratio, then prints historical cost transfers that remain conditional.

## Source inspection and available execution evidence

static-source-review; five-disposable-CLI-fixtures-passed-with-fixed-quantile-fallback; no-estimator-run.

Read-only linear parsing and statistics over a small saved table. The tested edition has 513 physical rows, 512 complete estimates and one incomplete line; no research campaign is performed.

## Declared inputs

**Sibling FRESH_2x128.tsv**: Read beside the script, not from the current directory or a CLI argument. The parser recognizes label, numeric seed and RESULT ... est value. It silently skips other lines and overwrites duplicate label/seed values.

**Labels and fixed seed blocks**: PUB_list170 and r37_p16_w4_rx; blocks 930000..930127 and 931000..931127. Per-block statistics use only those seeds, but the paired comparison uses every seed shared by both labels, including out-of-block seeds.

**SciPy availability**: Normally scipy.stats.t.ppf(0.99,df) supplies the quantile. If that import fails, only df=127 and df=255 have fallback constants. The publication's runtime tests force that fallback with python -S.

## Outputs and interpretation

**Block statistics**: Count, log2 of the linear mean, relative sample standard deviation, log2 of an approximate one-sided Student lower estimate and zero count. Invalid or nonpositive lower estimates are not guarded.

**Paired dq and standard error**: dq=log2(mean candidate / mean baseline). The delta-method standard error uses seed-paired normalized residuals. These calculations do not prove unbiasedness, independence or confidence coverage under the actual sampler.

**Projected claim lines**: Transfers based on hard-coded historical constants and floors. They are descriptive conditional calculations, not accepted, exact or premise-free contest prices.

## Worked examples

### Reproduce the archived fallback arithmetic

Working directory: A scratch directory containing copies of fresh_summ.py and FRESH_2x128.tsv

```text
python -S -B fresh_summ.py
```

-S disables site packages, deliberately selecting the fixed-quantile fallback tested by the publication. Without -S an installed SciPy may supply slightly different quantiles.

Expected: Four n=128 block rows, two n=256 aggregate rows, and paired n=256 with dq=-0.00570 and se=0.00613. The candidate's saved mean is lower; no positive gain is established. Historical projected-claim lines must not be promoted to a new exact price.

## Failure modes

- Duplicate label/seed records silently replace earlier estimates; the independent data guide rejects duplicates instead.
- The source parser does not validate inner seed values or the estimator's NP/M/MT parameters.
- A missing seed changes the sample size and can make the fixed-quantile fallback raise KeyError.
- An out-of-block common seed enters the paired calculation, unlike the fixed-block means; 257 paired seeds are unsupported by the fallback.
- Empty or singleton samples, zero means, nonpositive lower estimates and nonfinite inputs are not robustly handled.
- A confidence formula or printed claim is not a proved sampler law or complete operation ledger.
