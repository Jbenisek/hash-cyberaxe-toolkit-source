Inspect the W17 cancellation in the pinned chi37 characteristic and distinguish a broader uniform-word model from the restricted 32-member W15 list. This is a local schedule argument, not a compression collision or complete attack probability.

## Declared inputs

**Pinned characteristic**: chi37.txt supplies signed word masks, the saved example and 32 W15 list members. The wrapper verifies its SHA-256, distinct list members and bit-29 orientation before accepting the local result.

**Original working directory**: The original reads ../../chi/chi37.txt relative to the current directory, not its script location. Use the portable wrapper, which creates the required temporary layout. There are no command-line parameters.

**Difference convention**: Modular difference means x minus y modulo 2^32; u is (1,0), n is (0,1). W15 changes x bit 29 from one to zero. The W10 difference is fff80c00.

## Outputs and interpretation

**Exact admitted-list count**: All 32 distinct pinned W15 values give sigma1(x)-sigma1(y)=0007f400; adding fff80c00 cancels W17 when W1 and W2 agree.

**Broader uniform-word law**: Three disjoint parities, x4=x6, x22!=x31 and x20=x27, give rank three. Exactly 8/64 assignments of those six bits, or 2^28 of 2^31 complete words with x29=1, cancel. The admitted list already enforces them, so this is not a new factor-eight gain.

**Eight control results**: Pinned enumeration, independent bit formula and rank, missing input, wrong working directory, short list, wrong orientation, duplicates and a changed early row. The last two expose acceptance limitations in the original script, not rejected inputs.

## Failure modes

- The original accepts 32 copies of a single valid word and prints 32/32. The wrapper separately checks distinctness of the pinned list.
- A changed early word can produce a nonzero modular difference followed by a hard-coded zero label while the process exits successfully. The script is not a validator of arbitrary characteristics.
- Early-word equalities prove zero contributions at the named schedule taps, not unconditional equality of all later rows.
- Do not multiply the broader one-eighth rate into a model already restricted to the successful 32-member list.
- The characteristic credits an existing published lineage. This lesson does not claim its discovery, full compression feasibility or a new round record.
- Optimized Python disables assertions; the replay wrapper rejects -O. Match the external ZIP digest as well as its internal manifest.
