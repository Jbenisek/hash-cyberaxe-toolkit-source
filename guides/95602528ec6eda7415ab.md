# Common-chain A/E diagnostic: the exact verify_cv contract

Source: `research/tools/kepler_tools/verify_cv.py`

Public SHA-256: `14c13e6fd869b2bbe6a11ce1bca888961da413064f3bcbd726ac9405c4e9b14a`

## Reviewed usage contract

Check A/E characteristic rows on two messages evaluated from the first recorded chain. This historical interface verifies those rows; full collision verification additionally requires the complete output predicate.

## Source inspection and available execution evidence

static-source-review; source-specific-disposable-CLI-controls-passed; no-solver-run.

Always two 39-step traces plus the requested prefix comparisons. No solver campaign or attack pricing.

## Declared inputs

**PAIR.txt**: M1: W= and M2: W= fields of sixteen lowercase eight-hex-digit words; a chain= or cv= field of eight words is required. Only the first chain is used for both sides.

**TRAIL.xml and k**: Complete unique A/E rows 0..k with recognized 32-symbol masks. Require 0<=k<=38. This CLI always computes 39 steps, even though its constant table contains 64 words.

## Outputs and interpretation

**Chain notice, row diagnostics and total A/E violations**: Only -,u,n,x,0,1 constrain bits; other symbols are skipped. W masks and digest equality are not checked.

**WITNESS VERIFIED / WITNESS INVALID**: Both labels exit zero. A negative endpoint passes vacuously. Neither label is a legal-message collision certificate.

## Worked examples

### Read a historical A/E prefix diagnostic

Working directory: Directory containing verify_cv.py, PAIR.txt and TRAIL.xml

```text
python -B verify_cv.py PAIR.txt TRAIL.xml 16
```

Expected: Checks A/E rows 0..16 from the first chain and prints the violation count. Check the numerical pair, both chain declarations and W masks separately; use the newer A/E/W diagnostic when its additional predicate is required.

## Failure modes

- A second chain is ignored; reject unequal initial contexts before this common-chain check.
- Contradictory W rows do not affect its verdict.
- k=39 raises IndexError; k<0 skips every condition.
- Unknown symbols are unconstrained and INVALID exits zero.
- No distinctness, padding, full-output or encoder-validity certificate is produced.
