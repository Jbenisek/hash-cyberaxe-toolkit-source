## Failure modes

- Running -O disables assertions and is explicitly rejected.
- Changing any kit input invalidates its manifest and is rejected.
- The pass-grouping optimum is limited to the supplied finite cost model.
- Do not confuse an exact integer counter with a verified transform implementation or attack success-rate proof.
- The L>=2m guard is essential; L>=2m-1 alone does not contain the final requested coordinate.
- Historical baseline proofs and package builders are not included or launched. Preserve akki-rock05, bradcush and Jbenisek lineage from the README.
