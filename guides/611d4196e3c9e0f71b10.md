## Reviewed usage contract

Historical batch launcher for six sparse schedule shapes. Its first-word nontriviality repair does not repair the state window: it still forces A/E equal before row 8. The downloadable lesson generates only temporary templates; it never executes this shell script or a solver.

## Source inspection and available execution evidence

static-source-review; not-replayed-for-publication.

No attack price follows from this launcher. A configured per-solver timeout does not bound total preprocessing or simultaneous CPU/memory use. The finite teaching replay generates 22 small text files and runs no solver.

## Declared inputs

**Historical environment**: Bash/WSL with hardcoded workspace directories, Python, mk_tap.py, sha_cnf.py, Kissat, timeout, nice and awk. The headerless sparse42_fix4.tsv rows supply R, base-word indices and kick indices.

**Constraint recipe**: Permitted W rows are the union of words and kicks. State freedom starts at 8 and ends at R-5. REQUIRE_DIFF takes the first listed base word, not a computed minimum. An earlier first required word makes the first transition inconsistent when all prior schedule words and initial states agree.

## Outputs and interpretation

**Files and concurrency**: Truncates SPARSE42_FIX4_RESULTS.tsv before building. Overwrites generated per-shape XML/CNF/map files. Builds each shape serially, then starts a background solver with a 1200-second timeout; it has no global worker cap.

**Status semantics**: MK_FAIL/CNF_FAIL identify build failures. Solver s-lines are extracted through awk. With no such line, the summary says UNKNOWN_cap1200, which does not distinguish timeout, execution failure or missing output. A DONE line counts summary rows, not verified collisions or checked proofs.

## Failure modes

- Do not execute the historical launcher to test this lesson: it overwrites results and fans out workers.
- UNSAT for an accidentally contradictory template is not an impossibility theorem for the intended shape.
- UNKNOWN_cap1200 is not a certified timeout status.
- Allowed W differences are not all required differences, and equal initial states do not mean the standard IV.
- There is no independent witness replay or UNSAT certificate check in this launcher.
