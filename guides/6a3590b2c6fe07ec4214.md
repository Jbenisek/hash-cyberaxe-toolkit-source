## Reviewed usage contract

Recalculate seven historical batch-pricing rows with exact integers and Fraction values. The script proves the upward four-decimal exponent rounding for its embedded primitive-operation totals; it does not establish the scientific premises behind those totals.

## Source inspection and available execution evidence

static-source-review; seven-fixed-input-arithmetic-rows-replayed.

Seven finite big-integer exponent comparisons, with no collision search. Fresh local replay completed in about 0.34 seconds; that is an observed runtime, not a machine-independent cost bound. The script's 430 divisor and 2^38 allowance belong to its historical charging model.

## Declared inputs

**Invocation**: No CLI arguments, stdin or input files. Use Python 3 to run this exact source snapshot.

**Embedded constants**: The file contains historical AP and pkg16f work terms, batch bounds, allowances and hypothetical replacement costs. Changing them creates a different calculation and may invalidate the scientific interpretation.

**up4(O)**: For the displayed fixed rows, O is the nonnegative primitive-operation total. The calculator forms N=O+430*2^38 and finds the least integer k with N^10000 <= 430^10000*2^k. The reported exponent is k/10000.

## Outputs and interpretation

**Seven result rows**: Each prints a primitive-operation total O, a floating-point display of log2(O/430+2^38), and an upward-rounded four-decimal exponent backed by adjacent integer inequality checks.

**Control row**: The pkg16f filed arithmetic row has O=6080111232731503201962608254739666816 and rounds upward to 113.4454.

**Exit status**: Exit 0 with all internal assertions passing in the fresh replay. A failed assertion means the fixed arithmetic consistency checks did not pass; do not silently replace the expected value.

**Filesystem and network effects**: The reviewed script writes only to stdout and imports fractions/math from the standard library. The -B flag prevents bytecode caching.

## Failure modes

- The rows labelled conditional, mean or LEAD retain those qualifications after successful arithmetic replay.
- The assumed 512-per-c allowance in the final lead is not derived by this program.
- Repricing an embedded hypothetical program is not proof that the replacement algorithm exists or has the assumed success law.
- The printed integer-test notation refers to the exponent k/10000; the actual comparison is implemented exactly in up4.
- These historical prices are not asserted to be the current leaderboard, accepted scores or complete contemporary cost models.
