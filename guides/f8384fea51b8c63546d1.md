## Reviewed usage contract

Inspect a rejected support-parity heuristic and reproduce why neither its SAT/UNSAT labels nor its connected groups certify the modular schedule.

## Source inspection and available execution evidence

static-source-review; eight-extracted-definitions-and-three-finite-counterexamples-checked; original-driver-and-solvers-not-run.

Small text and parity processing for a bounded characteristic. No search cost or attack price is established.

## Declared inputs

**BASE.xml**: Text rows of signed W patterns, parsed by a layout-sensitive regular expression. A/E constraints are ignored by the model.

**MODEL**: Default word mode builds support parities. MODEL=bit uses a reachability filter, not an exact modular carry model.

**Optional MIRROR.tsv paths**: Historical SAT/UNSAT labels are scored as supplied, without checking certificates. With no explicit matching paths the source attempts local auto-discovery.

## Outputs and interpretation

**Printed free/pinned/group and SAT/UNSAT predictions**: Heuristic model output only. A connected parity component does not mean all its variables must flip together.

**Agreement percentages**: Agreement with supplied labels only; not predictive validity, solver evidence or a probability of finding a collision.

## Worked examples

### Replay the schedule counterexample by hand

Working directory: No project tree needed

```text
A=(1,1,0,...,0); B=(0,...,0); W16=sigma1(W14)+W9+sigma0(W1)+W0 mod 2^32
```

Expected: W16(A)=0x02004001, W16(B)=0. Give A/E wildcard masks and W the exact pair signs. Swapping A and B satisfies all mirrored W signs, while the model equation x0+x1+x16=0 incorrectly rejects that mirror.

## Failure modes

- Both modes label a concrete satisfiable mirrored 17-word schedule UNSAT.
- Union-find connectivity is weaker than equality of coordinates on a kernel.
- The returned nullspace basis can be wrong because dependent pivots are not fully propagated; classify discards it, so this separate defect does not explain the direct membership prediction.
- base and xexp are predicted SAT without verifying their actual feasibility.
- Missing maps yield no accuracy denominator; a scoring heading alone is not an evaluated result.
- The parser and sign support ignore full state, message-domain and carry constraints.
