# BLAKE3 B5v2: inspect the revised probability model

## Reviewed usage contract

B5v2 changes four planning/probability functions while preserving the five-round arithmetic and layout. This is a model revision, not a faster hash circuit. The guide separates exact integer accounting, a reproduced numerical certificate and the still-separate proof obligations. Historical predecessor attributions remain in the original source.

## Source inspection and available execution evidence

static-source-review; Own-pin finite kernel/interface controls and selected-parameter Appendix A replay. No default optimization, original selftest, retained generator or collision campaign..

One finite circuit construction/allocation under a ten-second subprocess limit. The separate fixed Appendix A replay used a 30-second limit and did not optimize.

## Declared inputs

**Target and representation**: Five-round BLAKE3, unit 1054; a 64-byte block of 32 zero bytes followed by little-endian x, standard IV, counter 0, length 64, flags 11. The walk stores f XOR q in 256 bit-sliced lanes. The circuit has 31,606 binary operations, 771 allocated registers and 16 compiler chunks.

**Record fields**: Ten-byte endpoint records contain 64-bit keys and 16-bit trail identifiers. The bounded controls reject upper overflow, but negative -1 aliases maximal unsigned fields. Validate nonnegative inputs and retain the final full recovery check after a truncated-key match.

**Model revision**: Only _zq, _lossq, _b256 and plan differ at function level from B5. The midpoint sum is replaced by a 20,000-panel trapezoid calculation with margins; cut-loss terms and planning threshold also change. The helper is still a fixed full-width model. Fresh circuit equivalence alone does not justify these formulas.

**Selected replay inputs**: The audit supplies Tp=138 and B=1390213469953000000000000000000000000. It extracts the reviewed Appendix A definitions before the top-level optimizer, then executes only the fixed calculation in a disposable subprocess with a 30-second limit. The local appA.out corroboration is historical, not a public runtime dependency.

**Compiler-only example**: The example below is simpler: it builds and allocates the exact downloaded circuit and prints its size. It never launches the original campaign, a default planner or the embedded C generator.

## Outputs and interpretation

**Fresh finite arithmetic and interfaces**: 256 fixed lanes match the independent reference; two known answers, allocation/chunk controls, two rejected mutations and masked-walk/packed-recovery fixtures pass. Four field roundtrips, two overflow rejections, six artificial one-batch branches and four finite common-endpoint recovery graphs also pass. Graph call counts are 5, 7, 4 and 2; these are not real BLAKE3 witnesses.

**Exact historical ledger at supplied parameters**: N=45128498571539297266031696934851486991500 operations. The independently expanded parts equal the source ledger. Decimal log2(N/1054)=125.009503424636190207063586148195144289713333800179154766507, which reproduces historical upward rounding to 125.00951. This is the time component, not a total memory-adjusted or newly accepted score.

**Reproduced helper, not an independent theorem**: Program and Appendix float helpers both return 0.3900000010010407. The selected decimal helper returns 0.390000004922061386474912943115367469800683174527196172547812, matching the saved output prefix. This replays the proposed numerical certificate; it does not independently prove all error enclosures, the random-function theorem or transfer to fixed-target BLAKE3.

**Same layout; different proof obligations**: Declared setup/search phases remain 1,325,688 and 1,363,176 bytes. The allocation limit is 1,363,240 bytes. Packed recovery still costs 1,072 operations plus 64 extra per batch. The different model does not change these structural counts, but its selected run length changes the total ledger.

**Safe probability interpretation**: For positive parameters, the exponential of the convex quadratic/hinge expression used here is convex, so exact trapezoids bound that integral above. That mathematical fact alone does not certify floating-point rounding, all other loss terms or the target-transfer premise. Keep those boundaries when citing the historical number.

## Failure modes

- Do not call the changed model a faster circuit; the kernel is unchanged.
- Do not equate a reproduced numerical certificate with an independently audited theorem.
- Do not present 125.00951 as the complete memory-adjusted score.
- Do not run the Appendix A file wholesale merely to reproduce the supplied-parameter result.
- Truncated-key matches and synthetic graph pairs are not full target collisions.
- Keep the machine convention and target-transfer premise separate from finite implementation controls.
