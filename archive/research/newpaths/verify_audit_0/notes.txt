2. LOOK CLOSURE IS ORDER-INDEPENDENT  (DERIVED, then COMPUTED on small systems)
  If z is look-acceptable for a plain-closed K, it is look-acceptable (or already known) for every plain-closed
  K' >= K: replay z's propagation chain s0=z,s1..; every chain equation of a word not in K' is newly covered, and
  the first chain word that IS in K' has a chain equation still containing an earlier unknown word (else it would
  be derivable from K), which supplies the extra equation.  So the closure is monotone; BFS over flats and bf.c's
  sorted-DFS with skip-if-known are both exact.
