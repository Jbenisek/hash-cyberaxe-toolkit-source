# A7: low algebraic degree in the cube and member bits (ANGLES.md section 7), 2026-10-09

**Verdict: CLOSED for this angle's mechanism.** The literal kill rule ("no bit has degree <= 3") is not met: many bits have
degree <= 3. But every one of them reads at most 5 cube variables (at most 8 for a rule-A parity). The angle needs
degree <= 2 in 10 or more variables. That happened in **0 of 2,192** bit-frames (MEASURED). The low degree comes from
carry chains: degree is almost the number of variables read (nvars - deg is 0, 1 or 2 for every h1 and omega bit).
So "solve instead of filter" adds nothing beyond a lookup on <= 8 bits. The full rule-A flag, the E1 word and Y9 are
close to full degree.

## What was computed (MEASURED; anf_degree.py, Windows Python 3.14 + numpy, CPU only, 29 s)
- Part C, the pkg16f counter cube. For 16 random frames (halfsearch.ctr_outer, key = Y12, Q, w5, C2.b1, C2.c1, e1), these
  are Boolean functions of the 21 free c1 bits (ctr_c1 order): all 32 bits of h1 = ROR(Y14 ^ e1, 16), the 8 rule-A
  parities (stage A), the rule-A pass flag, and all 32 bits of the E1 word (zero = hit, the residual). The ANF comes
  from a Moebius transform over 2^21 points.
- Part Y, the pkg21 member of outer.py / memberloop.py. For 16 random contexts, omega = Y3 + y + w8 (the input of test (2))
  and Y9 (the input of test (1)) are functions of the 19 member bits. The ANF comes from a Moebius transform over 2^19 points.
- Reference checks: the numpy lanes match the scalar formula 0/1,024 mismatches and ctr_lane (full 2-round compressions)
  0/112. The member lanes match outer.scalar_outer 0/1,024.

## Not tested (plainly)
- The ANF over the low 20 counter bits (ANGLES text). Each counter value is a new frame, so this needs the frame builder's
  whole chain, which this test did not run.
- Degree after a linear change of variables, or of linear combinations of output bits. The c1 -> h1 map uses only
  additions of constants, XORs and rotations, so any gain there is carry-guessing (angles 3 and 5), not low degree.

## Side finding (MEASURED, neutral.py, 256 frames, 157 s): cube bits neutral for stage A
- Exact over all 2^21 members, a flip of the bit never changes the rule-A pass flag. 177 of 256 frames are non-empty
  (30.9% empty). Neutral bits per non-empty frame: 0 in 41 frames, 1 to 3 in 23, 4 or more in 113. The neutral bits
  are mostly cube indices 6 to 9 (c1 positions 11 to 14), each in 107 to 119 frames.
- DERIVED upper bound: if each frame's neutral bits were known at no cost, stage A could be run on 2^(21 - k) members
  and copied. Stage-A work falls to 0.528 of today's, counting empty frames at full cost. That is <= 0.92 bit, on the
  stage-A part only. GUESS: an exact per-frame neutrality test from the frame constants (a carry automaton) is cheap. It
  is untested. This is a lead for angle 5, not for angle 7.
- A6 says "pass counts are multiples of 1,024". That is not reproduced here: 125 of 177 non-empty counts are not
  multiples of 1,024 (2-adic valuation 0 to 15). The mean is 8,809 passes per frame (A6: 8,213), which is consistent.
