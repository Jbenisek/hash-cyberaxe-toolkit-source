## 1. Same mechanism, special case, or new? Same.

- DERIVED (proof 8, step CT): the trial lines `K0.a1 = IV0 + IV4 + w0`, `t = ROL(K0.d1,16) XOR K0.a1` and
  `w1 = S0 - K0.a1 - K0.b1` are the mover identity. K0.d1, K0.b1 and S0 are step-CO names, fixed for each outer
  step, so `K0.a1 XOR t` is constant (ANGLES: `a1' = a1 xor (i xor i')`) and `K0.a1 + w1` is constant (ANGLES:
  `m1' = m1 + a1 - a1'`). Lemma CT (c): the trials of one outer step differ only in w0, w1 and t. These are ANGLES's
  m0, m1 and i.
- What moves and what stays fixed is the same in both. Fixed: the post-round-0 state X, w2..w15, v13 = 0 and
  flags 3. Moved: w0, w1 and the counter. In round 1 the movers change C2's outputs Y2, Y6, Y10, Y14, and E0
  through m1. Downstream, E1 and E3 change through Y6 and Y14, which are the step-CT lines.
- An ANGLES base is a counter-construction outer step (7 outer words + member y). Movers per base: 2^32 - 1, with
  t != 0 forced in both by the flags (rule CS).
- MEASURED (64 bases x 2^10 movers, flags 3, t and t' uniform below 2^32): 65,536 of 65,536 checks passed for each
  of the following.
  - (a) The post-round-0 state is unchanged. This is ANGLES M1 again, on the counter instance.
  - (b) Each mover (w0', w1', t') equals `ctr_trial(o, c1')` exactly, with c1' read back through C2 and E1
    (Lemma IP). So the mover family of a base is exactly the trial family of its outer step.
  - (c) Y4, Y9, omega, C2.b1, C2.c1, Y1 and Y12 are unchanged.
  - (d) c1' lies in Q* if and only if E1's A/B b-difference is beta* (Theorem C (iv)). 28 of the 65,536 movers
    were in Q*, against 32.0 expected at 2^-11.
- New trials per base: **0**. Of the 2^32 movers, exactly the 2^21 with c1 in Q* carry beta*. The construction
  already takes all of them, by construction rather than by filtering. The other 2^32 - 2^21 fail beta* (DERIVED
  "exactly when", and confirmed by (d)). Using them would need a different E1 difference, which is a different
  characteristic, not angle 1. The front line does not list even the 2^21: the joint solver (9.4) solves over
  h = E3.h1, a permutation of the mover index (Lemma IP), and step 3 certifies (Q).
- So ANGLES's "2^21 now, 2^27.06 needed" cannot be closed by movers: the 2^21 already is the mover yield after
  beta*. The EDGES curve is cited there and was not rechecked here.
- The two ANGLES extras that differ add nothing:
  - Short messages (i < 2^7) would only shrink the set. t is a permutation of c1 and cannot be chosen (proof 8);
    2^7 movers x 2^-11 gives about 2^-4 useful movers per base (DERIVED).
  - A v13 (K1) mover needs t >= 2^32, i.e. messages of 4 TiB or more. It moves w2 and w3, and with them C0 and C1:
    Y4 (the member) and Y9. That is a change of outer step, not a trial, and outer steps are already free
    (7 uniform words; a run uses 2^69.5 of them) (DERIVED).

  The first three, 83.3% of the charge, are invariant work and are already paid once per base. The mover dimension
  appears only in the solver, which solves for h rather than enumerating, and in step 3's (Q), (A), (C) and (T) on
  at most 16 roots. No per-mover condition is left to hoist: **saving 0**.
- **Old counter batch (9.2, `ctr_batch`; not run by the front line).** Stage A is 41 units per 7 movers: loop 4,
  cube word to Y14 10, z 6, rule A 21 (`CTR_TABLE`). Every line from the cube word onward reads the mover word
  ROL(E1.d1,16). The invariants (Y12, -Y1-w12, C2.b1, -C2.c1, and rule A's word V of Y4) are already per-base
  register constants (`ctr_memory`, `CTR_RESIDENT`). Invariant stage-A conditions: 0. Invariant ops: only the
  4-unit loop bookkeeping (about 10%), which is not a condition.
- ANGLES's two examples agree with this split. e1 = Y3 + Y4 + m15 is the member-list word E (per base), and
  E1.a1 = Y1 + Y6 + m12 is a step-CT line (per mover).
- The root instance's stage A (6.5; counter 0, flags 11) has no movers without the switch to flags 3. That switch
  is the counter instance.
