1. WHY
   The judge refused entry 9a9f3b7b (research/dossiers/9a9f3b7b; status not_evaluable, reasons
   lane_*/heuristic/H7prime-ak-cluster): "No experiment, implementation, or scaled model measures the frame-level
   factorial-moment bound asserted by H7prime-ak-cluster"; "has no supporting proof or experiment at frame scale".
   The premise (research/pkg16/pkg/blake3-r2/claim.json, score-critical): for a frame of proof.md Section 9 (the six
   outer words C0.d1, D2.b1, S11, S4, X9, w6 and a member y of the sub-class, drawn uniformly), W = the number of
   listed good valid trials over all 2^32 values of D2.a1 and all 2^21 members of Q*; assume E[W(W-1)] <= 0.0065 E[W].
   E[W] = 2^32 (2^21 - 1) p_good = 2^-38.47 with p_good = (5/7) 1033 2^27 / 2^128 = 2^-91.473 (H1prime-rate). The
   premise allows a cross-step clustering factor over independence of ALLOWED = 0.0065 / ((2^32-1) 2^21 p_good) =
   2^31.207 (the within-step part, Rw (2^21-1) p_good, is negligible unless Rw > 2^63). This run measures, in the
   pattern of research/impl/memberloop/prereg8, the per-frame clustering of the deepest measurable trial events of the
   frame search, with a control arm of fresh frames and two generators.

2. DEFINITIONS (frozen in perframe.cu and, independently, in ref_pf.py; constants and step CO generated from the filed
   program research/pkg16/pkg/blake3-r2/experiments/halfsearch.py by gen_consts.py)
   Frame: eight generator words; the first six are C0.d1, D2.b1, S11, S4, X9, w6, the seventh is unused, the eighth mod
     2^17 is the member number, y = halfsearch.class_member (the sub-class of 131,072). (As step 1 of proof.md 9.1.)
   Outer step: frame + g = D2.a1; step CO = halfsearch.ctr_outer. Trial: outer step + c1 in Q* = {c1 : c1 AND
     0e09818b = 02008000}; step CT = halfsearch.ctr_trial; t = T0; t = 0 trials are not valid: excluded from every
     event and counted. E1 = G(Y1, Y6, Y11/Y11', Y12, w12, w5 / w5 + delta) and E3 = G(Y3/Y3', Y4, Y9, Y14, 0, w8) on
     messages A and B. tau, eps, beta = XOR differences of E1's a output, c output, first-half b; eps3, j1, j3 = E3's
     a output, first-half c, c output; h1 = E3's first-half d on A; r = XOR of chaining-value word 1 = tau ^ j3.
   Sampling in a frame: G = 2^19 values g_i = bg XOR Lg.gray(i) (Lg nineteen uniform 32-bit columns conditioned on rank
     19, bg uniform), so every ordered pair of distinct g is sampled with equal probability; k = K_TRIALS values c1_j =
     CV | deposit(b XOR L.gray(j)) (L ten uniform 21-bit columns of rank 10, b uniform), the SAME k c1 in every outer
     step of the frame, so every ordered pair of distinct c1 is equally likely and same-c1 cross-step pairs are
     sampled 2^21/k times more often than in the frame (weighted back in section 3).
   EVENTS (T7 = the seven outcomes of beta*: taus 175020a0 185020a0 275020a0 285020a0 385020a0 675020a0 685020a0,
   eps* = 6e21be55, beta* = 18b0e098, G(tau) = ROL(tau ^ ROR(tau,1), 12)):
     (d) T2 per outer step: automaton (2) of halfsearch on Y3 + Y4 + w8 nonzero
     (d) P  per outer step: mask1(Y9) AND mask2(Y3 + Y4 + w8) nonzero (the filter pass, seven taus)
     (a0) E1a per trial: tau in T7
     (a) E1L per trial: tau in T7, eps = eps*, beta = beta*: the E1-listed event of 9.6 = the roots of the frame search
     (b) E3P per trial: eps3 = eps* and j1 = G(tau_q) for a tau_q of T7: (J2) and (J1) of the E3 side of a listed good
         trial; every E3P must lie in a step whose pass mask has bit q (checked)
     (c) R16, R24 per trial: the low 16, 24 bits of r zero
     (e) RA per trial: rule A on h1 (halfsearch.rule_a), a condition of a good trial
   ARMS: ctx (arm 0): one frame per block (slot 0xFFFFFFFF), 2^19 outer steps. ctrl (arm 1): the same g walk and c1
     set, fresh frame words for every outer step (slot = step index).
   GENERATORS: G1 Philox4x32-10 (= cuRAND = Random123 KAT), key = first 8 bytes of keyhex(kind, 1), counter (slot,
     block, arm, 2*purpose + h); G2 SHA-256 of bytes.fromhex(keyhex(kind, 2)) || BE32(arm, block, slot, purpose).
     g subspace: slot 0xFFFFFFFE purposes 3a..3a+2; c1 subspace: slot 0xFFFFFFFD purpose a (attempt a).

3. STATISTICS (frozen in analyse_pf.py; per arm, pooled over G1 and G2, and per generator; unit = frame)
   Per frame and trial event: n, SW = sum over outer steps of n_s(n_s-1), SC = sum over the k c1 of n_c(n_c-1).
   m = G (step events) or G k (trial events, nominal); N, p = N/(C m), v = 1 + (var - m p (1-p))/mean^2,
   F = sum n(n-1)/N, E0 = (m-1)p, R = F/E0; Rw = (SW/N)/((k-1)p); Rx_same = (SC/N)/((G-1)p);
   Rx_diff = ((SF-SW-SC)/N)/((G-1)(k-1)p); Rx = 2^-21 Rx_same + (1-2^-21) Rx_diff (the cross-step factor of the whole
   frame); Rx_pool = ((SF-SW)/N)/((G-1)k p) (descriptive); rho_x_full = Rx (2^32-1) 2^21 p, rho_w_full = Rw (2^21-1) p.
   SE: delete-one-group jackknife, 128 contiguous groups of the arm's frames (G1 blocks ascending, then G2).
   ctx - ctrl differences with SEs in quadrature; pair counts per stratum against no clustering; exact Poisson upper
   limits (95 %, 99.865 %) of a stratum's ratio when its pairs are fewer than 30; mean/nominal (P CTR_SHARE/2^64, E1L
   53 2^-38). Descriptive: pairs_pf.py lists the E1L and E3P pairs by stratum and whether same-c1 pairs share K_O(g).

8. RUN PROTOCOL AND DECISION RULE
   `bash start_main.sh` (= `python3 -B run_main.py main`) checks this file against PREREG.sha256, reads K_TRIALS,
   SEG_FRAMES, MAX_SEGMENTS, CAP_SECONDS from it and runs the segments once. The only stop is the wall-time rule of
   section 6; no frame or run is excluded. If a run crashes the main run is void, reported, and repeated once in full
   with attempt 2. Then `bash post.sh`: sha256sum -c PREREG.sha256; analyse_pf.py main main_out.json --prereg;
   ref_pf.py hits (every E1L and E3P hit of the main run recomputed by real compressions; any failure is reported);
   pairs_pf.py.
   PRIMARY, per deep event E1L, E3P, R24, ctx arm: U = (1 - 2^-21) U_diff + 2^-21 U_same, with U_s = Rx_s + 3 SE
     (jackknife) when the stratum has >= 30 pairs, else its exact 99.865 % Poisson limit. BOUNDED-2 iff U <= 2,
     BOUNDED-16 iff U <= 16; reported with log2 U and its margin to ALLOWED = 2^31.207.
   VERDICT on H7prime-ak-cluster at the deepest reachable events: SUPPORTED iff every primary U <= 16 (a margin of at
     least 27.2 bits); NOT SUPPORTED iff some primary U > 2^31.207; WEAK otherwise.
   SECONDARY: NO-EXCESS iff |Rx(ctx) - Rx(ctrl)| <= 3 SE (per primary event); the same for Rx_same, Rx_diff, Rw, v and
     R of every event; per generator; checks: beta = beta* on every valid trial, no E3P outside a pass with its bit,
     row shapes, per-c1 totals = n, hits, hashes.
   SCOPE: E1L (the E1 half of a listed good trial: 96 bits, the roots of the frame search) and E3P ((J1) and (J2) of
   the E3 half) are measured separately; a listed good trial (2^-91.47 per trial, 2^-38.47 per frame) is not reachable.
   The run bounds the per-frame clustering of the deepest reachable partial events, not that of listed good trials; the
   transfer to the joint event remains the premise. Pseudo-random generators stand for uniform words; counter
   instance only; the GPU may be shared.
