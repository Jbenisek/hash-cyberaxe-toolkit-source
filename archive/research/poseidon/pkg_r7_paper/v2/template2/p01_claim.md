# Orchard Poseidon, r = @@R@@: algebraic collision algorithm with a worst-case cost bound (not executed at r = @@R@@). Two free rounds on a line, odd part about a random centre, half-gcd, at most @@K@@ coin centres

## 1. Claim

Target: `poseidon-orchard-r@@R@@-prefix-v1` (Orchard P128Pow5T3 over Pallas, full rounds 0-3, @@PARTIAL@@, full rounds 60-63,
state `[nk, rho, 2^65]`, digest = coordinate 0). Output: two distinct canonical pairs `(nk, rho)` with equal digests.

This entry is an algorithm with a worst-case cost bound and a success probability derived from ONE declared score-critical premise, `H-GEOM-w@@W@@` (section 9.1). The program was not run at r = @@R@@: one trial needs @@TRIAL_MEM@@ of live memory and @@TRIAL_WORK@@. The premise is a statement about the number of rational components of two algebraic curves attached to the fixed digest polynomial; section 9.2 derives the success probability from it with explicit constants.

* The algorithm (sections 2-4): draw a centre `c` uniformly from `F_p` with the cost model's random words, build the odd part `g_c` of the line polynomial by evaluation and one inverse transform, compute `G = gcd(g_c, S^((p-1)/2) - 1)` by a power-mod and a half-gcd, and if `G` has degree between 1 and `DMAX = @@DMAX@@` isolate a root of `G` with at most `Z = @@Z@@` coin rounds, take its square root, map back and verify the two inputs with the reference hash. At most `K = @@K@@` centres are tried, one after the other, on a single thread; the first verified collision stops the run. A run that finds none exits with status 2 and no output.
* `success_probability = @@P_SUCC@@`: a lower bound for the probability, over the program's own random words, that one of the `K` centres yields a verified collision on the fixed target. It is derived in section 9.2 from `H-GEOM-w@@W@@` and from proved statements: `P >= 1 - (1 - q)^K - K (eps_iso + eps_coin)` with `q = @@Q@@` (the bound for `P(1 <= k <= DMAX)` of section 9.2), `K = @@K@@`, `eps_iso < 2^-48`, `eps_coin < 2^-65`. The exact value is `@@P_SUCC_EXACT@@`.
* `time_log2 = @@TIME_LOG2@@` in target-compression units (`C(@@R@@) = @@C@@` primitive operations per unit): the worst-case operation count of the program, derived in section 6 as `T_fixed + K * T_trial` with every trial charged in full (including stage E at its cap of `Z` rounds at degree `DMAX`), `N_max = @@N_BOUND@@` operations, `log2(N_max / @@C@@) = @@TIME_LOG2_EXACT@@`, rounded up at five decimals. The cap on the number of trials is tested at the start of every trial; the isolation rounds are capped; every other loop has a length fixed by `m` (section 4.1). The offline derivation of the literals of the source (section 6.2) is bounded by @@OFFLINE_BOUND@@ operations; that bound is inside `N_max` and is declared as `preprocessing_log2 = @@PREP_LOG2@@` (one quantity, not added twice).
* `memory_log2_bytes = @@MEM_LOG2@@` (`@@MEM_BYTES@@` bytes, section 7): the code image, the arena that serves every buffer of the largest trial, the twiddle table and the stack bound. The normalized cost is `@@TIME_LOG2@@ + @@MEM_PEN@@ = @@SCORE@@`.
