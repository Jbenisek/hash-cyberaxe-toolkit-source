# Orchard Poseidon, r = @@R@@: algebraic collision algorithm. Two free rounds on a line, odd part about a random centre, half-gcd, at most @@K@@ centres

## 1. Claim

Target: `poseidon-orchard-r@@R@@-prefix-v1` (Orchard P128Pow5T3 over Pallas, full rounds 0-3, @@PARTIAL@@, full rounds 60-63,
state `[nk, rho, 2^65]`, digest = coordinate 0). Output: two distinct canonical pairs `(nk, rho)` with equal digests.

This entry is an algorithm (sections 2-4) with a worst-case operation count (sections 6-7) and a success probability derived from the score-critical premise `H-GEOM-w@@W@@` (section 9.1), a statement about the number of rational components of two algebraic curves attached to the fixed digest polynomial; section 9.2 derives the success probability from it with explicit constants.

* The algorithm: draw a centre `c` uniformly from `F_p` from fresh random words, build the odd part `g_c` of the line polynomial by evaluation and one inverse transform, compute `G = gcd(g_c, S^((p-1)/2) - 1)` by a power-mod and a half-gcd, and if `G` has degree between 1 and `DMAX = @@DMAX@@` isolate a root of `G` in at most `Z = @@Z@@` rounds, take its square root, map back and verify the two inputs with the reference hash. At most `K = @@K@@` centres are tried, one after the other, on a single thread; the first verified collision ends the algorithm.
* `success_probability = @@P_SUCC@@`: a lower bound for the probability, over the algorithm's fresh random words, that one of the `K` centres yields a verified collision on the fixed target: `P >= 1 - (1 - q)^K - K (eps_iso + eps_coin)` with `q = @@Q@@` (the bound for `P(1 <= k <= DMAX)` of section 9.2), `K = @@K@@`, `eps_iso < 2^-48`, `eps_coin < 2^-65`. The exact value is `@@P_SUCC_EXACT@@`.
* `time_log2 = @@TIME_LOG2@@` in target-compression units (`C(@@R@@) = @@C@@` primitive operations per unit): the worst-case operation count, derived in section 6 as `T_fixed + K * T_trial` with every trial charged in full (including stage E at its cap of `Z` rounds at degree `DMAX`), `N_max = @@N_BOUND@@` operations, `log2(N_max / @@C@@) = @@TIME_LOG2_EXACT@@`, rounded up at five decimals. The offline derivation of the constants (section 6.2) is bounded by @@OFFLINE_BOUND@@ operations; that bound is inside `N_max` and is declared as `preprocessing_log2 = @@PREP_LOG2@@` (one quantity, not added twice).
* `memory_log2_bytes = @@MEM_LOG2@@` (`@@MEM_BYTES@@` bytes, section 7). The normalized cost is `@@TIME_LOG2@@ + @@MEM_PEN@@ = @@SCORE@@`.
* Coins: the only randomness is the cost model's random-word primitive, fresh and independent, one operation per word; `@@COIN_WORDS@@` words at most (section 4.1.1), and a draw of five words costs @@COIN_DRAW_COST@@ operations. There is no other input and no advice (`nonuniform_advice_log2_bytes = 0`).
* The method (two free rounds on a special line, so that the digest is a univariate polynomial of degree `5^@@DEXP@@`, then the roots of its odd part about a centre, found with a gcd against `S^((p-1)/2) - 1`) is the two-round bypass of Bariant, Bouvier, Leurent and Perrin (ToSC 2022/3) combined with a Cantor-Zassenhaus style root search; credits are in the submission note.
* Declared heuristics (claim.json): `H-GEOM-w@@W@@` (score-critical, section 9.1) and `H1-charging-rules` (supporting: the charging rules of section 4.2, the normal Euclidean sequences of stage D, the register file of 18 words, and the arena of section 7). Everything else is proved or counted here: the line construction, the degree of `F`, the odd-part reduction, the collision criterion (sections 2, 3, 5), the Weil-Cauchy-Schwarz reduction (section 9.2), the isolation lemma (section 9.4), the independence of the centres, the cap rules and every operation count (sections 4, 6, 7).
