## 2. Certificates: not a route
verifier/certificates.py: each certificate needs message_a != message_b, digest(a) == digest(b) == expected_digest (full 32-byte digest, hex), type hash-collision-witness-v2, target_profile; at most 16 (MAX_CERTIFICATES), 1 MiB per file, 4 MiB package. Any mismatch raises VerificationError (the whole package fails), no partial/masked mode exists. Tested: a w=20 agreement pair in a manifest (staged copy of the r56 package in a scratch slot) gives
`VerificationError certificate w20: message_b digest 6f119a7a... does not match expected 6f117a69...`.
So reduced-width agreement pairs are NOT an allowed certificate format; only full collisions of the selected target (POSR4M's r4 collision is such). Shipping them elsewhere (extra files) is refused by intake (undeclared files); inside proof.md they are prose the judge cannot recompute (Poseidon is not recomputable by a reviewer) = untrusted, same as our earlier "untrusted reduced-width checks".
Also a certificate could never show hazard behaviour: it shows existence of a pair, not the rate (the rate is the H1 content).
