# Impact of ruling 748a4266 (exec_r2_line, NOT_EVALUABLE) on our other Poseidon entries

Checked: only the named files of each directory (line.c, ntt.c, gen_kern.py, split.c, hgcd.c, lean.c, common.c, nolibc.c, single.c, proof_template_exec.md, note_template.md). Read-only; nothing in those directories was changed.

The ruling had three findings:
- (1) lane_cost, H2-runtime-completeness (the blocker): posix_memalign/free instruction counts omitted; the xorshift generator charged as one random word.
- (2) the generator: `rnd_fp254` charges `X.rnd += 1; X.ctl += 2` for four xorshift64 steps (three shifts, three XORs and the assignments each).
- (3) lane_evaluability (material): index and control integer multiplication, division and modulo charged as 1 operation; they are not in the primitive list.

What this means:
- exec_r2d and single_r1_exec4 passed review with the same flaws; a stricter judge or a re-review of the board could rule them the way 748a4266 was ruled. They are exposed on (1), (2) and (3). If either is re-judged or challenged, the fix is the one in `exec_r2_line2` (arena allocator, `nolibc.c`, stage I, xorshift charged per operation, no integer `*` `/` `%`), which needs one full re-run per entry (about 25 minutes at r = 2 for exec_r2d; the r = 1 entries are smaller).
- single_r1_exec6 is exposed on (2) and (3) and on the report-formatting divisions; it is not exposed on (1). A NOT_EVALUABLE ruling for it would name the same two points. Its fix is the `exec_r2_line2` source changes ported to the r = 1 odd-part code (the same files).
- The exec_r2_line2 changes that are shared code (identical in all four directories before this fix): `common.h` (`udivmod`, `NEW_FP`), `ntt.c`, `gen_kern.py` (regenerates `ntt_kern.h`, `ntt_a3.h`), `line.c` (`rnd_fp254`), `split.c` (`split_tries`), `hgcd.c` (`k1`), `lean.c` (breakdown line, `ebit` comment, `CTL(500)`), `nolibc.c` (division-free `printf`, no `log2`, no `%f`), `single.c` (no clock, no float, stage I constants), `pallas.c` (unused `fp_from_hex`/`fp_to_hex` removed), `build_package.py`, the two templates.
- Measured cost of the fix: N = 215,719,166,817 against 212,794,851,943 for exec_r2_line (+1.37%, +0.0198 in log2; most of it the counted allocator walk in the half-gcd stage, the stage I constant is 3.38 M). Memory 87,941,284 bytes (log2 26.391) against 91,558,513 (26.449): the arena is inside the image and tuned. Score 36.03014 against 36.06845.
