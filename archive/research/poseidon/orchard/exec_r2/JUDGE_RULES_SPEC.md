## 2. Gap that is NOT a rule (INFERRED, engine)
- Half-gcd: rival 6,563,144,944 at deg 390,624 (ledger) vs our 1,508,601,759 at deg 39,062. Scaling the rival engine by degree ratio 0.1 with a ~0.9 log factor gives ~0.55-0.65 G at 39,062 (INFERRED); our D is ~2.5x that. Our r2 lean half-gcd at m = 195,312 was 19.04 G (counter_poseidon_r2.md, SEEN) vs rival 6.56 G at twice the degree, i.e. about 6x. The lever is our "other" column in D (1,119 M other vs 390 M arith), mostly address ops and loop control.
- Transform kernels: at the same size rival cached squaring = 25.76 M units at deg 39,062 (my closed form, see section 3) vs our 36.95 M; most of that gap is the cached spectra (memory), not the per-butterfly rules.
- Check of my closed form: reproduces PR 37 s.4.2 exactly at deg 390,624 (241,785,065.5 vs published 241,785,063; uncached = cached + CACHE(Dq+1,N1) = 22,377,953, matched).

## 4. Caveats
- Judge status: only 186052af and PR 19 are judge-valid in our snapshot; PR 37 status unknown, so rows quoted only from PR 37 (QN1 caching, 4.5 per-butterfly text is identical in PR 18) are secondary evidence. Rows 3, 4, 5, 7, 8 (INVQ), 9, 11, 12, 16 are in the judge-valid PR 18 text.
- Rival numbers are closed forms; ours are counters. A judge that accepted "1/2 unit per butterfly is conservative" has not been shown to accept a compiled program with the address-op removed; keep the stronger wording and say plainly that the register-block assumption (18 words) stays disclosed.
- Memory note of ours: judges required charging dispatch and full source; do not drop row 13 charges just because rivals do not state them.
