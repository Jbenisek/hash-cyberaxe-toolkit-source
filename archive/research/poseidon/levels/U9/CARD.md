## 0. The one-paragraph picture

R0 re-labels the input (bijection nk,rho -> A,B). In AB-coordinates each round multiplies the lane degree by 5 (full or partial), the lanes are **full triangles** of monomials at every depth we could reach, and the (nk,rho) support is the AB-triangle pushed through the binomial substitution, i.e. exactly {ceil(i/5)+ceil(j/5) <= N} (proved upper bound, boundary layer proved nonzero, interior observed). The **top-degree structure is much poorer than the lower layers**: the first partial round (R4) removes lanes 1,2 from the top form, after which all three lanes have top forms that are scalar multiples of ONE polynomial, so the output's top form is a perfect power c * Phi3^(5^5) (r=1) or ^(5^6) (r=2) of a single degree-125 form Phi3 that has no root on the projective line over F_p. Consequences: every F_p-line has exactly the generic degree (5^9 / 5^10), per-variable degree equals total degree, and no direction survives. The only sparse objects found are the two axes through the centre (a=0, b=0), where the map is a function of a^5 (flat to order 5, line polynomials 80% zero coefficients), and the isolated rank-1 points of the Jacobian.

## 4. Question 5 (placed here because it explains 1 and 3) - the top homogeneous form

## 5. Question 4 - the (nk,rho) support law

Law: lane polynomial of AB-degree N has (nk,rho)-support {(i,j) : ceil(i/5) + ceil(j/5) <= N}, hence |support| = **1 + 10 N + 25 N (N-1) / 2** monomials (w(0)=1 value of i with ceil 0, 5 values of i for each ceil >= 1).

**Proof of the containment and of the boundary layer** [PROVED]. Let q(A,B) = sum q_mn A^m B^n with q_mn = 0 for m+n > N. Substitute A = (x+c)^5, B = (y+d)^5, x = nk, y = rho, c = c0,0, d = c0,1 (both nonzero, G4). Then
q = sum_{i,j} x^i y^j * E_ij, E_ij = sum_{m >= ceil(i/5), n >= ceil(j/5), m+n <= N} q_mn * C(5m,i) C(5n,j) c^(5m-i) d^(5n-j),
and C(5m,i) is nonzero mod p because 5m < p. Consequences:
 1. E_ij = 0 unless ceil(i/5) + ceil(j/5) <= N (the sum is empty). This is the support bound.
 2. If ceil(i/5) + ceil(j/5) = N the sum has exactly one term, (m,n) = (ceil(i/5), ceil(j/5)), so E_ij != 0 iff q_mn != 0. **So the whole boundary layer of the law is present as soon as the AB-triangle is full there** (in particular the corners x^(5N), y^(5N): this is the per-variable degree statement of section 3).
 3. For ceil(i/5) + ceil(j/5) < N the sum has several terms; E_ij != 0 is not forced (needs no accidental cancellation). **OBSERVED** all present: real field nk-coordinates R1: 301 (law 301), R2: 7751 (law 7751), **R3: 195001 (law 195001, symmetric difference 0 on all three lanes)** - this R3 count was a prediction in the atlas ("PREDICTION, untested"), now confirmed (`u9_support_nk_r1_R3_output.txt`). Toy q=1,000,003 nk-coordinates R4 (N = 625): law predicts 4,881,251 monomials, found 4,881,247 on every lane, 0 outside the law, 4 missing = accidental zeros (expectation 4.9) (`u9_toy_nk_R4_output.txt`).
 Also toy R1..R3 for 12 (q,seed,r) runs: 0 monomials outside the law ever (36 lane-cells at each of R1,R2,R3), and 0 missing except 4 lane-cells at R3 that miss exactly 1 (accidental, expectation 0.2-0.25 per lane) (`u9_toy_support_output.txt`).
- Counts for the table (formula, DERIVED): N = 1, 5, 25, 125, 625, 3125, 15625, 78125, 390625 gives 11, 301, 7751, 195001, 4,881,251, 122,062,501, 3,051,718,751, 76,293,750,001, 1.907e12 monomials; the law misses exactly 10 N monomials of the full (nk,rho)-triangle of degree 5N (N=5: 351-301 = 50; N=125: 196251-195001 = 1250), a relative gap of about 0.8/N. The output (nk,rho) polynomial for r=1 has N = 5^8 = 390625, i.e. 1.907e12 monomials in its law-support, and 5^9 total degree.
- **Observed for the three lanes at partial rounds**: in nk-coordinates the lane polynomials after R4 (toy) have the same 4 missing monomials on all three lanes - the zeros sit where only w^5 contributes (top part), where the lanes are proportional (section 4). ## 7. Question 7 - where the Jacobian rank drops to 1, and how far it propagates

State Jacobian J_k = d(state after round k)/d(A,B) is 3 x 2. J_0 = M[:, 0..1] (rank 2 always, MDS). **Round recursion** J_k = M * diag(d_k) * J_(k-1), d_k = 5 s^4 per lane (full round) resp. (5 s_0^4, 1, 1) (partial), s = state after the constant addition.

- **PROVED** rank J_k <= rank J_(k-1) (M invertible, D can only lower rank): **the rank never recovers.** The sets Z_k = {(A,B) : rank J_k <= 1} are nested, Z_0 subset Z_1 subset ... Observed nested = True in all 20 toy runs (`u9_toy_jac_output.txt`).
- **PROVED** (nk,rho)-coordinates: J_nk = J_AB * diag(5 a^4, 5 b^4), so **the two lines a = 0 and b = 0 (2p-1 points) have rank <= 1 for all rounds**: along a = 0 the nk-derivative of the whole state is 0 (and also derivatives of orders 2,3,4: the map is a function of a^5). At the crossing a = b = 0 the Jacobian is 0 (rank 0). These are the "points where rank drops" at R0; they propagate to every later round and to the output. (Note: they are not rank drops in AB-coordinates; (nk,rho) -> (A,B) is not a local diffeomorphism there.)
- **PROVED + OBSERVED, isolated points at R1**: rank J_1 <= 1 in AB-coordinates iff two of the three S-box inputs of R1 vanish (one linear equation system in (A,B) per pair of lanes; any single vanishing lane leaves two independent rows because the 2x2 minors of M are nonzero). Exactly **3 points**, one per pair (0,1), (0,2), (1,2), all F_p-rational. Toy: exactly 3 points after R1 in all 20 runs (q = 257, 769, 1009, 4099, seeds 1-3, r = 1,2).
- **OBSERVED, real field** (`u9_jac_real.py`, `u9_jac_real_output.txt`): common F_p-rational zeros of two S-box inputs (resultant interpolation in A, gcd in B, then forward-mode Jacobian through all later rounds and comparison of the output with `hash_reduced` at the pulled-back (nk,rho)): **R1: 3 points, R2: 2 points (pairs (0,2), (1,2); pair (0,1) none), R3: 6 points (2 per pair)** - 11 points in all, with rank 2 until the round of the drop and **rank 1 at every later round up to the output, for r=1 and r=2**. Pair resultants have degree 1, 25, 625.
- **Not done on the real field**: points where exactly one S-box input vanishes and the other two Jacobian rows are dependent (curve intersect curve, appears from R2 on); R4 and R5 (partial rounds, only lane 0 can vanish, so only that kind of point occurs); R60..R62 double zeros (resultant degrees 5^k squared: infeasible here).
- **OBSERVED toy** (`u9_toy_jac.py`, exhaustive over all q^2 points): number of (A,B) points with rank <= 1 after the last round: 32-50 for q = 257, 36-44 for q = 769, 36-43 for q = 1009, 33 and 38 for q = 4099: **bounded, does not grow with q**; they come from three sources that appear in the cause column: >= 2 S-box inputs zero (new points), exactly one zero with dependent rows (new points), and inherited. After R2 3-15 points, after R3 8-21, after R4 9-21 (mostly inherited). No toy run had a rank-0 point in AB-coordinates.
- Critical points of the output (both partial derivatives of lane 0 zero): **0-3** points per toy run (e.g. 3, 2, 3, 3, 2, 2 at q = 257); almost never at a rank-1 point of the state (2 of 20 runs had one). So rank-1 states do not make the scalar hash critical in general.

## 9. Boring details worth keeping

- The Kronecker stride W must exceed the largest x-degree of every intermediate product; AB-coordinates at R5 need W = 3126.
- python-flint 0.9.0 `fmpz_mod_poly` multiplication **segfaults** when the product length exceeds roughly 4-8 million 255-bit coefficients (2e6 x 2e6 square works, 4.2e6 square dies with exit 139, silently when run in a background chain). First attempts of R5 (AB), R60 (AB) and the r=2 line to R63 died this way; `u9lib.Ring.mul` now splits products into 2^20-coefficient blocks (block product checked equal to the direct product on a 300k x 200k example). The failed attempts are kept as `*_FAILED_*` files.
- Wall clock: nk-coordinates R3 (Kronecker, W = 626): 17 s; AB R4: 6 s; AB R5 (4.9M monomials x 3 lanes): 340 s; r=1 exact line to degree 1,953,125: 66 s.

- DERIVED (R4 in AB-coordinates): lane i = M_i0 * w + M_i1 s_1 + M_i2 s_2 with deg w = 625 and deg s_1, s_2 = 125, so the three lanes are proportional in the top 500 layers (total degree 625 down to 126) and differ only in degrees <= 125 (the lowest 20% of the degree range, which is the region of the 126x127/2 = 8001 lowest monomials). **OBSERVED** (`u9_proportional_depth.py`): lane1 - (M_10/M_00) lane0 and lane2 - (M_20/M_00) lane0 at R4 both have exactly 8001 monomials = the full triangle of degree 125 (max degree 125, min 0).

## 10. The five most useful / surprising observations

1. **The output's top form is a perfect power, c * Phi3^(3125) (r=1) or ^(15625) (r=2), of one degree-125 form with irreducible factors of degrees 4, 9, 112 and no F_p-root.** The first partial round makes all three lanes proportional at the top and they stay so. So the three-lane width disappears from the top structure; every F_p-line has exactly the generic degree (a closed formula, checked against the exact degree-1953125 polynomial), and per-variable degree equals total degree for every fixed other variable.
2. **The only forced zero coefficients are on the two centre axes a = 0 and b = 0**: there the map is a function of a^5; line polynomials have exactly 80% zeros at every round (only exponents divisible by 5), and the whole state is flat to order 5 in that direction. Move the base point off the centre and the same line is dense.
3. **Support law proved at the boundary, observed inside**: the (nk,rho) support of an AB-degree-N lane is {ceil(i/5)+ceil(j/5) <= N}, count 1 + 10N + 25N(N-1)/2; the boundary layer is automatically nonzero (a one-term sum), which gives the corner monomials; the atlas prediction 195001 at R3 is confirmed on the real field and 4,881,251 at R4 is confirmed up to 4 accidental zeros on a toy.
4. **Full triangle in AB-coordinates holds at every layer we can reach**: all of R4 and R5 exactly, the lowest 121 layers through R63, the top layers through R63, and the toy zero counts equal Poisson(N/q) - so "dense" is generic, not structural. Real polynomials at p ~ 2^254 have no accidental zeros.
5. **Rank-1 points**: the state Jacobian can never regain rank; the two axes are rank <= 1 for all rounds; apart from them there are exactly 3 isolated points at R1, and on the real field 11 F_p-rational ones by R3, all verified to stay at rank 1 through the output. On toys their number saturates (about 30-50) instead of growing with q.

## 11. Questions this raised (open)

1. Exact count of the other kind of rank-1 point on the real field (one S-box input zero and the other two Jacobian rows dependent) and the double-zero points in R4/R5 and R60-R62: resultants of degree 625 are easy, degree 15625 and above need a smarter elimination (the sets are nested, so is the count simply the number of F_p-points of a fixed variety?).
2. Why do the lane forms factor with degrees 1+4, 1+5+19, 4+9+112 (R1,R2,R3)? Random-polynomial statistics say nothing special; check whether the quartic at R1 is an accident (degree 5 form with one root) or comes from the 5th-power structure (Phi_5 is irreducible over F_p of degree 4).
3. Behaviour on curves of higher degree (conics, A = a0 + u t + w t^2): can the top form be lowered by composition with degree-2 curves (the top form of the image curve is Phi3 evaluated on the curve's leading part), and are there curves whose images have markedly fewer monomials?
4. At R4 the lanes are proportional down to degree 126 (measured: the difference is exactly the degree-125 triangle). What is the depth at R60..R63, where lanes get re-mixed by full rounds (each full round re-introduces a lane-specific part)? Answer needs layer-wise differences of the exact polynomials (too big here; the truncated top-layer ring could do it).
5. Zero coefficients of the output at the intermediate homogeneous layers between the top s and the bottom 120: all tested layers are full; is there any layer pattern (e.g. in the layers just above the point where lanes decouple)?
6. The flaky run (section 6): a rare silent wrong product in flint 0.9.0 or a one-off? Re-run the centre-axis check several times and with `LIMIT` smaller to see if the blocked path changes anything; until then every deep result needs its value check (it has one).
7. Do the 3 irreducible factors of Phi3 (degrees 4, 9, 112) and the roots of the lane forms at R1/R2 interact with the 11 rank-1 points (the fibres of the two structures)?
