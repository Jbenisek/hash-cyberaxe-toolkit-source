# Decomposition test, inner degree 625 and 3125 (2026-10-12)
Target: Orchard Poseidon line polynomial F_j(W), r=1, deg n=5^7=78125, p = Pallas base prime, j=0,1,2.
Code: math/decomp_625_3125.py, reusing levels/decomp/decomp.py (the earlier 5/25/125 test; line model and constants from there).
Method (Kozen-Landau, tame p>n): top d coefficients of monic F -> m-th root series (m=n/d) -> candidate H (monic, H(0)=0);
then full H-adic digit expansion of F by divide and conquer; decomposition iff every digit is constant (F=G(H) exactly).
Tame case: a decomposition over the algebraic closure is already defined over F_p (Fried-MacRae), so testing over F_p suffices.
Also, in the tame case H is unique given F and its degree, so one candidate per degree is exhaustive.

Validation: planted F'=G'(H') with random monic H' (deg 625, G' deg 125) and H' (deg 3125, G' deg 25): H' recovered exactly, 0 nonconstant digits.
Negative control (one low coefficient of F' perturbed): test reports a nonconstant digit (rejects).
