Design of the 8-byte length family (lengths 55 and 63), pin H8
================================================================
Research note, 2026-10-06.  Nothing here is filed.  This replaces, for delta = +-8,
the construction of research\pkg5\blake3-r2\proof.md sections 3, 4 and 6.1; the names,
the call layout (K0..K3, D0..D3 of round 0; C0..C3, E0..E3 of round 1) and the inverse
"Fact 1" (proof.md section 2) are used unchanged.  The reference is h8_ref.py; the
solve order is arrangement.txt (= research\night\main\h8_arrangement.txt, the certificate
of basis_sat.py with PIN_EXTRA=w14).  Verification counts are in h8_ref_check.txt.

0.  Constants of pin H8
-----------------------
    K  = IV[2] + IV[6] = 5bf2cd1d
    lengths 55 and 63, 55 XOR 63 = 8
    X3  = 29d4fa98   X7  = bee3af28   X11 = 44036000   X15 = 40c58500
    W4  = 97475638   W13 = 0007c006   (top byte of W13 is 0)
    W4' = ((K+W4) XOR 8) - K = 97475640      delta = W4 - W4' = fffffff8  (= -8)
    C3 = G(X3,X7,X11,X15; w4, W13):
        w4=W4 :  a out Y3  = 8127c181   c out Y11  = 7af77f38   b out 76f7aefd  d out bbfbdffe
        w4=W4':  a out Y3' = 7edf3e7e   c out Y11' = 850000c3   b out 76f7aefd  d out bbfbdffe
        DY3 = Y3' - Y3 = fdb77cfd        D11 = Y11' - Y11 = 0a08818b
    eta = 830303cf.    (All finite computations; pin_facts.py and h8_ref verify them.)

1.  Length cancellation  (Lemma L', replaces proof.md Lemma L)
--------------------------------------------------------------
Lemma L'.  K2 with block length 55 and words (w4, w5) leaves the same four state words
as K2 with block length 63 and words (w4', w5'), where
        w4' = ((K + w4) XOR 8) - K,   w5' = w5 + w4 - w4'.
Proof.  Let a1 = K + w4.  In the second run the first assignment gives K + w4' = a1 XOR 8,
so the second gives ROR(63 XOR (a1 XOR 8), 16) = ROR(55 XOR a1, 16) because 63 XOR 8 = 55;
hence the same d1.  The third and fourth assignments depend only on d1 and constants.
The fifth gives (a1 XOR 8) + b1 + w5' = a1 + b1 + w5 since w5' - w5 = w4 - w4' =
a1 - (a1 XOR 8).  The last three depend only on values already equal.  QED.
(K2 is the only round-0 call that reads v[14] = block length, w4 or w5, and it overwrites
v[14]; so two messages sharing every word but w4, w5 related this way have the same S and
the same X.  This needs delta even, which 8 is; proof.md section 8 / NOTES section 9.)

Honest byte strings.  A is the first 55 bytes of w0..w15, B the first 63 bytes with
w4->w4', w5->w5'.  B's bytes 55..62 must be zero: byte 55 is the top byte of w13, bytes
56..59 are w14, bytes 60..62 are the low three bytes of w15.  So the family forces
        w14 = 0,  w15 = 0,  top byte of w13 = 0,
in addition to proof.md's w15 = 0.  W13 = 0007c006 has top byte 0; w14 and w15 are pinned
to 0 in the construction.  A's bytes 55..63 are zero fill automatically.

2.  The pinned call and the free half  (Lemmas P', H', replace Fact P and Lemma H)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Lemma P'.  For the H8 constants C3 gives equal b output (76f7aefd) and equal d output
(bbfbdffe) for w4 = W4 and w4 = W4', and differs in the a and c outputs as in section 0.
Lemma H'.  Two 2-round compressions with equal state X after round 0, equal message words
except w4, w5, with (X3,X7,X11,X15) the pin, w13 = W13 and w4 in {W4, W4'}, agree on digest
words 0, 2, 5, 7.
Proof (identical to proof.md Lemma H).  C0, C1, C2 read neither w4 nor w5 and act on equal
states, so they agree.  C3 leaves equal v[7], v[15] by Lemma P', so Y agrees except maybe
Y[3], Y[11].  E0 = G(0,5,10,15; w1,w11) and E2 = G(2,7,8,13; w9,w14) read none of Y[3],
Y[11], w4, w5 (w14 = 0 is a shared constant), so Z[0],Z[5],Z[10],Z[15] and Z[2],Z[7],Z[8],
Z[13] agree, and o0,o2,o5,o7 use only those.  QED.
The residual R = (o1^o1', o3^o3', o4^o4', o6^o6') comes from E1 and E3 only; a collision is
R = 0.  E1 = G(Y1,Y6,Y11,Y12; w12,w5), E3 = G(Y3,Y4,Y9,Y14; w15,w8), with Y11->Y11',
Y3->Y3', w5->w5' on message B.

3.  The class  (Lemma Q', replaces proof.md Lemma Q)
----------------------------------------------------
w15 = 0, so E3's first assignment is e1 = Y3 + Y4 (message A) and e1' = Y3' + Y4 (B); its
second is h1 = ROR(Y14 ^ e1, 16).  Since Y14 is equal for A and B, the XOR difference of
E3's first-half d value is eta(Y4) = ROR((Y3+Y4) XOR (Y3+Y4+DY3), 16), a function of Y4.
Lemma Q'.  Fix eta = 830303cf.  The class {Y4 : eta(Y4) = eta} is
        { Y4 : (Y3 + Y4) AND 03cf8303 = 030c0303 },
with Y4 = e1 - Y3.  It has 2^19 members (13 bits of e1 fixed, 19 free, bit 31 among them).
Proof (as proof.md Lemma Q).  x = ROL(eta,16) = 03cf8303 has bit 31 = 0, so m = x.  Y4 is
in the class iff e1 XOR (e1+DY3) = x, i.e. (e1 XOR x) - e1 = DY3, i.e. 2((NOT e1) AND x) =
DY3 + x = 01870000, which is even; halving gives (NOT e1) AND m = 00c38000, all of whose
bits lie in m, so (e1 AND m) = (NOT 00c38000) AND m = 030c0303 and the other 19 bits of e1
are free.  QED.
Member k (0 <= k < 2^19): e1 carries the bits of k at its free positions in increasing
order; Y4 = e1 - Y3.  (h8_ref.class_member, in_class, class_mask_value.)

4.  Context  (step S1', Lemma S', replaces proof.md section 4 and Lemma S)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
The arrangement of basis_sat.py (PIN_EXTRA=w14) has eight free basis words
        C0.c1  C0.d1  S11  S4  X13  X14  Y4  w0 ,
inner word Y4.  Seven are the context (step S1'); Y4 is the trial.  Each line below is one
assignment of one call (round 0, or C0/C1/C2) solved by Fact 1 for the word on its left;
pinned words (X3,X7,X11,X15,w4=W4,w13=W13,w14=0,w15=0,Y3,Y11) appear as constants and are
not listed.  Order is topological (the solver's certificate).  Fifty lines, Y4-independent:

  K0.a1 = IV0 + IV4 + w0

  K0.d1 = ROR(0 ^ K0.a1, 16)
  K0.c1 = IV0 + K0.d1

  S8    = ROL(S4,7) ^ K0.b1

Lemma S'.  For every assignment of the seven context words, round 0 with block length 55
on the sixteen words (w6,w7 and the twelve above, plus pinned w4,w13,w14,w15) gives the
column state S and state X with (X3,X7,X11,X15) the pin, w4 = W4, w13 = W13, w14 = w15 = 0.
Proof.  Every line is Fact 1 (proof.md section 2) for one assignment of one round-0 (or
C0/C1/C2) call, solved for the name on its left; the pinned outputs X3,X7,X11,X15 and
pinned words w4,w13,w14,w15 enter their own assignment-equations as constants (line
D3.b1 = X3 - D3.a1 - 0 is the X3-output equation; D2.a1 = X2 - D2.b1 - W13 the w13 equation),
so forward evaluation reproduces them.  The order is acyclic and every word is solved once
(the SAT certificate, arrangement.txt).  QED.  (Verified: on 200000 instances the forward
round 0 of A and of B, lengths 55 and 63, gives equal X with the pin -- r0eq, pin, zword
all 200000/200000 in h8_ref_check.txt.)

Lemma F' (D0,D1,D3).  With the new X0,X10 and the words w8,w9 above, D0 = G(S0,S5,S10,S15)
has outputs (X0,X5,X10,X15); with the new X1,X6 and w10,w11, D1 = G(S1,S6,S11,S12) has
outputs (X1,X6,X11,X12); D3 is untouched (its words w14=w15=0 and inputs are the context's),
so it keeps (X3,X4,X9,X14).  Lemma Y' (C0).  C0 = G(X0,X4,X8,X12; w2,w6) has b output Y4 = y
and d output Y12.  Lemma K'.  K3 is unchanged (w6,w7 fixed), so S3,S7,S11,S15 are the
context's.  Lemma T' (whole trial).  Round 0 with length 55 on the trial's words gives the
context's S (K3 unchanged) and its X with X0,X1,X5,X6,X10,X12 replaced; in round 1 C0 has b
output Y4 = y.  With Lemmas L',H' the pair A,B agrees on digest words 0,2,5,7, and for y in
the class the XOR difference of E3's first-half d value is eta.
Proof.  Each line is Fact 1 for one assignment of D0,D1,C0,C1,C2 (and the reduced E1,E3),
solved for the name on its left; D2,D3,K0,K1,K2,K3 are not touched because none of their
words or inputs is reassigned.  So round 0 maps the trial's words to the stated S and X.
Lemma L' then gives equal X for B.  The hypotheses of Lemma H' hold, giving the four free
digest words; Lemma Q' gives the eta for y in the class.  QED.  (Verified: on 200000

6.  Loop structure and cost (in G-call assignments)
---------------------------------------------------
Outer loop -- context, six words (C0.c1, C0.d1, S11, S4, X13, w0): solve the 31 context
lines that are not downstream of X14.  Cost 31 assignments, once.
Middle loop -- the word X14: solve the 19 context lines downstream of X14
(D3.d1, S9, K1.c1, S14, K1.d1, S10, X9, K1.a1, X4, w2, S6, C0.b1, K1.b1, S2, S1, S5, w12,
w3, w5).  Cost 19 assignments per X14, and every X14 gives a fresh context carrying all
2^19 class members.  (X14 is the cheapest of the seven -- the others cost 21 to 37; see the
dependency counts.  X14 feeds all 46 trial lines, so no member work can be hoisted above it.)
Inner loop -- the member Y4 (2^19 values): stage A solves the 19-assignment cone of E3.h1
to get h1, on which a rule-A-style bit test (not derived here, section 8) is applied; a
member that passes continues through the rest of the 46 trial lines for the full residual
(cones: E3.g1 33, E3.e2 37, E1.d1 33, E1.b1 34, E1.a2 35 assignments cumulative).  Only
w8,w9,w10,w11 among the message words change per member.  The dominant work is the 19-
assignment stage A per member, the same as the filed pkg5 trial (20 recomputed words
including Y4; NOTES section 7 addendum).

7.  Trial count
---------------
The map (eight basis words) -> (sixteen message words) is injective (the words determine the
whole computation, hence the basis), so distinct basis values give distinct messages A.  The
seven context words are free 32-bit words and Y4 ranges over the 2^19 class, so there are
2^(7*32) * 2^19 = 2^243 distinct half-colliding trials, well above 2^104.  In the loop above
one outer context (six words) supplies 2^32 (X14) * 2^19 (members) = 2^51 trials, and the
2^192 outer contexts give 2^192 * 2^51 = 2^243.  Distinctness within a context: two X14 give
different X9,X4,..; two members give different Y4.

8.  What is NOT established here
-------------------------------
- Lemmas S',T' (and F',Y',K') are argued as Fact 1 applied line by line plus the SAT
  acyclicity/completeness certificate (arrangement.txt), and verified on 200000 random
  instances; a hand transcription of all 96 per-line Fact-1 proofs in full prose is not
  written out.  The claim "round 0 forward reproduces the pins for EVERY basis value" rests
  on that certificate, not on an exhaustive proof, though it held on every instance tested.
- No rule-A analogue and no E1-test analogue (proof.md Lemmas A, N) are derived for this
  class: the per-member filter that makes the search cheap is NOT designed here.  Without it
  the inner loop has no early test.  This is the main missing piece for a package.
- The class rate (brief: 159,833,954 over the 1500 lightest betas, ~2 bits better than the
  old family's 36M) is NOT recomputed here; it comes from night_main scan files
  (pinH8_830303cf.total) that this task did not rerun.  No score/claim number is produced.
- The packed 7-lane batch and its operation/load count (proof.md 6.5), the success
  probability (section 7) and the charged time (section 8) are not ported.
- Only single-block messages (<=64 bytes) are implemented in hash2; that is all the family
  needs, and it equals the organizer verifier for all lengths 0..64, but it is not the full
  tree hash.
