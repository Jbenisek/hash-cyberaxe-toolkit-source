The historical note identifies ePrint 2026/232, *Collision Attacks on SHA-256 up to 37 Steps with Improved Trail Search*, by Zhuolong Zhang, Muzhou Li, Lei Gao and Meiqin Wang, with EUROCRYPT 2026 metadata. It reports that the accessible abstract described a local-collision search tool for message expansion and two improved Boolean-function models. The stated motivation was that earlier signed-differential encodings omitted relevant Boolean bit conditions. The abstract was described as claiming a first 37-step attack.

The original reviewer could not access that PDF through its web reader and expressly did not establish the precise message domain, IV, padding, feed-forward, target profile or complete price. This publication note preserves that limitation. The lead is a reason to study richer Boolean constraints, not evidence validating the project's unrelated support-only SAT probes or a 40-step ordinary collision.
