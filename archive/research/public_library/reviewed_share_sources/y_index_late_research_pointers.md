## Method leads preserved without promotion to a claim

The index reports a schedule solver repaired after failed blind calibration, then real member construction in about 0.1-0.3 seconds for it19 and 1.3-2 seconds for F1 on three seeds. Another pointer attributes new sparse-door members to using SAT-solved fresh-word conditions as seeds for GPU search; it reports 157 members with two independent checks across seven selected doors. These are leads to the corresponding implementation and verification records, not fresh performance measurements in this review.

The most consequential late pointer concerns a height-40 candidate called door 6, with support T = {4,5,7,...,15}, expanded lit set L = {16,25}, closure coordinate c26 and an observed member with 79 differing head bits. It reports a head-conditioned tail estimate around log2 probability -40.24 for rows 17-26, versus about -131 for it19 under its comparator. The head remains unpriced and no complete conforming head characteristic is supplied by the index. The apparent 91-bit tail advantage is not a 91-bit improvement in total attack time.

Later pointers expose that missing connection. Five promising tails pinned into the modular model were ruled out for middle windows 9-12 and 10-13 by reported algebraic or certificate-backed checks; later windows remained UNKNOWN. Signed-tail pins starting at row nine also refuted the tested samples, while looser starts remained open. Some other sparse doors had heavier weighted tail objectives despite satisfying the reported head indicator. The final lesson in the index is to co-design the characteristic before fixing member values or gluing independently generated head and tail states.

A whole-door signed-difference model was reported to accept the published control and door 6 quickly, but those unbounded characteristics failed the later value gate even for the control. Another attempt reused the historical MNS-style sparse-end formulation with message differences left open; its control pipeline still did not produce a value pair within the reported effort. These are calibration gaps, not an established construction.
