This is a dated visibility observation, not a claim that such artifacts never existed or remain unavailable today.

The scientific consequence is independent of website visibility: a score and an AI-review label do not supply a semantic decoder for a different SAT instance. To compare a local result with a public attack, the local artifact must identify the target, map model variables to messages, reconstruct those messages, independently verify the exact output relation, and count total work including failed attempts and all processors.
