The note attributes to Alamgir, Nejati and Bright's *SHA-256 Collision Attack with Programmatic SAT* a 38-step result using SAT plus computer algebra and a modified initialization vector. It also records the paper's reported pure-SAT reach of 28 steps in that work. Neither figure can be transferred to an ordinary standard-IV collision without the corresponding target and construction.

For a support-only SAT experiment, a satisfying assignment is not yet a message pair: it needs the semantic variable map, standard schedule expansion where required, legal domain/padding, exact starting state and final equality. Conversely, a solver timeout is not an impossibility result. This record supplies literature context, not a satisfiability verdict for the project's probes.
