## Benchmark facts that decide transfer (INFERRED consequence)
Target = 3-round BLAKE3 hash, fixed IV/flags, one chunk, 256-bit digest = v[0..7]^v[8..15] (feed-forward). Message block (16 words) is free; no key/state freedom (no free-start). Current 125.1 = birthday 2^128 shaved by a cheap packed circuit (595 ops vs C=638). A published attack transfers only if it yields a full 256-bit COLLISION with free message only at under ~2^125 priced ops. Distinguishers, partial/near collisions, and anything needing free state/constants do not transfer directly.

## Ranking of transferable ideas (best first, each with estimate and need)
1. Round-inversion / G-invertibility connector (row 1). Use free message words to force first-round G outputs into a chosen set so the remaining 2 rounds need fewer free words. Estimate: no score yet. To beat 125.1 an event needs probability above 2^-256 per trial by a factor that pays its price. Need: a multi-word connector with exact counts. INFERRED.
2. Linear-differential-of-addition prefilter (row 3). Estimate ~0 bits at r3 given own ATLAS/dig (385 families uniform within 0.5%). Need: a characteristic whose probability exceeds 2^-(bits saved). Unproven.
3. Hill-climbing for message values zeroing many digest bits (row 2). Gives a partial-collision stage feeding a birthday on the residual; pays only if filter cost is below the birthday saving. Weak at 3 rounds (diffusion already high).
4. Last-block / partial-CV control (own D220, motive from row 4, not literature). COMPUTED in D220: 125.09648, a constant-folding gain not an attack.
5. Rebound-style inbound phase on message words (row 7). No estimate; NEW GROUND if explored.
6. Rotational, internal differential, impossible differential, boomerang, PNB differential-linear: do not transfer (fixed IV breaks symmetry; distinguishers or key recovery only). These are CLAIMED-UNTESTED closures from reading, not our tests. A one-minute rotation-symmetry test (rotate message words, compare r3 outputs) would test the rotational closure; not run.

## Bottom line
INFERRED: no published result gives a 3-round BLAKE3 collision below birthday; all BLAKE/BLAKE2 results are distinguishers, impossible differentials or near-collisions with free chaining value/key. Remaining directions are ranks 1-3, all needing the same missing object: a message-only connector that gives a non-uniform digest-difference event at 3 rounds. Suggested next step: pure counting of how many of the 16 message words must be fixed to make rounds 1-2 invertible to a chosen intermediate state (no GPU).
